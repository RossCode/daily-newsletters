## Top Stories

### [Binance loses a record $6.3B in a hack blamed on North Korea](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/north-korean-hackers-blamed-for-stealing-record-6-3b-in-binance-breach/)
*(5 newsletters)*

Attackers drained about $6.3B from Binance in roughly 20 minutes on Thursday, the largest crypto theft on record, after apparently compromising an engineer's DevOps laptop. TRM and Chainalysis tie it to North Korea's TraderTraitor group (behind the 2025 Bybit theft), and about $3B was frozen in transit, including $2.3B by Tether and Circle. Binance halted withdrawals and says it will cover customer losses from reserves, which protects balances but leaves the stolen funds with the attackers.

### [Apple ships its first touchscreen MacBook Pro](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)
*(4 newsletters)*

Apple's new MacBook Pro adds touch and a tandem OLED "ProLED XDR" display, with up to 24 hours of battery, Wi-Fi 7, and availability from November 6; a new iMac and iPad mini launched alongside it. Coverage disagrees on chip and starting price (TLDR IT reports M5 Pro/Max from $2,899; CEO Report says M6 from $1,999), so check Apple's page before quoting specs. Developers are already showing how touch changes mobile app testing on the Mac.

### [Always-on personal agents become the new battleground](https://codenewsletter.ai/p/how-devs-are-using-always-on-personal-agents)
*(4 newsletters)*

OpenAI's dots, Meta's Muse, Anthropic's Claude Duet preview, and Grok Bot all give you an assistant with its own cloud computer that keeps working after you close the laptop and hands coding work off to Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor. OpenAI now lets you text your dot over iMessage and RCS, doubled usage limits on several plans, and plans a single token meter across Chat and Codex in December; Shopify's Shop Pay already works inside Muse and Claude Duet. The Code's advice is to scope each agent's credentials tightly and keep the merge button for yourself.

### [Big Tech's AI bill spooks investors as OpenAI's raise falls short](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-raises-less-than-half-of-30-billion-target-in-latest-round-91aecdc5)
*(4 newsletters)*

Cloud revenue soared (Google Cloud up 103% to $31B, Azure up 48%, AWS up 39%), but Google had its worst stock day ever, Amazon's trailing free cash flow hit negative $42.4B, and investors balked at roughly $750B in planned 2027 AI spending. Google also admitted AI Mode searches earn less ad revenue, while Meta grew revenue 26%. In that climate, OpenAI raised less than half of its $30B target at a $1.25T valuation, below the $1.4T it had hoped for.

### [Google's Project Suncatcher satellite answers its first prompt from orbit](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Google's prototype space data center ran Gemma 4 to write a haiku and beamed it down over Svalbard, which Sundar Pichai called a "hello world" moment. Power is the real constraint: only one of its four TPUs can run, in bursts of about 20 minutes. Two more capable satellites launching in 2027 will test the laser links a fleet would need to split one AI job.

### [Mistral releases weights for its trillion-parameter Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Mistral open-sourced Large 4, claiming the top open-weight score on agentic coding (Terminal-Bench 4.0 rose from 28% to 41%) and parity with GLM-5.3 on coding agents while using 47% fewer tokens per task. It targets self-hosted and defensive cybersecurity use, though closed models like Mythos 5.1 still lead. Running it yourself takes serious infrastructure, so check the license before commercial use.

### [Reddit will require logins as Google's AI answers cut its traffic](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/reddit-will-require-you-to-log-in-eventually-ceo-confirms/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Steve Huffman says Google's AI Mode no longer sends meaningful traffic to Reddit or anyone else, and Reddit is losing about 1M search referrals a week. Despite Q3 revenue up 53% to $895M, a soft Q4 forecast sent the stock down about 30%, and Reddit will start testing a logged-in-only experience outside the U.S. this quarter. It is a clear warning for any business that relies on Google for discovery.

### [Anthropic adds watermarks and renames its plans; Grok follows](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content)
*(2 newsletters)*

To comply with the EU AI Act, Anthropic will watermark text from Mythos 5, Opus 4.6 and 4.7, Sonnet 4.6, and all newer models, and SpaceX says it will invisibly watermark Grok API text starting November 7. Anthropic also renamed Max 5x to "Max" ($100) and Max 20x to "Ultra" ($200), and removed the 5-hour usage limit for verified Ultra accounts.

### [Cisco gives a 27-year-old unlimited tokens to rewrite its legacy code](https://fortune.com/2026/10/30/cisco-president-jeetu-patel-gen-z-27-year-old-unlimited-ai-tokens-rewrite-legacy-code-entry-level-jobs/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Cisco president Jeetu Patel put Padmanabhan Krishnamurthy, an ex-founder who joined through an acquisition, in charge of refactoring 98% of Cisco's legacy code within 24 months, with unlimited tokens and four weeks to deliver a proof of concept. Cisco says it wants to hire more people in his mold.

### [Anthropic files to go public under the ticker ANT](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-officially-files-for-its-public-debut/)
*(2 newsletters)*

The S-1 shows $16.3B in first-half 2026 revenue, up from $1.2B a year earlier, alongside an $80.7B net loss driven mostly by noncash accounting adjustments. The seven co-founders keep 50.1% of voting power through a "Founder LLC," and at current numbers the company's 5,300 employees would hold about $331B in stock.

### [Waymo's next robotaxi will be a Toyota bZ4X](https://electrek.co/2026/10/30/waymo-toyota-bz4x-robotaxi-japan-2027/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Toyota will build a custom 2027 bZ4X with the Waymo Driver installed on the line at its Takaoka plant. Testing starts in Japan next year, followed by the U.K. and other right-hand-drive markets.

### [Software pricing shifts from seats to work done](https://www.theregister.com/applications/2026/10/29/oracle-wants-to-bill-you-for-every-ai-task-not-just-every-seat/5306403)
*(2 newsletters)*

Oracle will add usage-based "AI units" to Fusion subscriptions, billing for each automated task on top of seats. TLDR Founders argues this is the broader trend: seat-based B2B pricing is fading as vendors charge for outcomes, which makes software budgets harder to forecast.
