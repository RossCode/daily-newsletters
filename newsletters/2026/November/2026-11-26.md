## Top Stories

### [The AI math avalanche keeps coming: OpenAI pins the matrix multiplication exponent at 9/4](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10379)
*(3 newsletters)*

OpenAI released nine more AI-written math papers, including a claimed proof that the exponent of matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4 over every field, which would settle how fast computers can ever multiply large matrices. Separately, an [Anthropic proof that VP does not equal VNP](https://www.friedgil.com/vpvnp) shows permanents cannot be computed with polynomial-size arithmetic circuits, and Google posted six papers from its Gemini 4-powered Cogentic research agent. The Rundown frames this as the month's dinner-table topic, alongside last week's Anthropic Riemann Hypothesis claim and a 16-year-old reporting 100+ unverified conjecture proofs with GPT-6.2 Astra.

### [AI agents are now running cyberattacks with almost no human input](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/amazon-says-hackers-ai-agents-found-and-exploited-0-day-with-little-human-input/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Amazon says agents on Cairn, an open-source pentesting framework, found and exploited a zero-day in a university medical imaging app from nothing but a goal prompt, and Palo Alto tracked a Cairn campaign that breached at least 10 of 19 targeted organizations. Google is issuing similar warnings about [autonomous AI attacks](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-autonomous-ai-cyberattacks/), and researchers traced an AI-assisted [Workday payroll-diversion campaign](https://www.reco.ai/blog/inside-a-machine-speed-workday-payroll-diversion-campaign) that changed direct deposits within seconds of login. The takeaway: patching and incident response now have to operate at machine speed.

### [Cognition ships SWE-2.1, closing much of the cyber gap with frontier labs](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2-1)
*(3 newsletters)*

Built with RL on top of Kimi K3, SWE-2.1 roughly doubles its predecessor on Terminal-Bench 4.1 (50.0) and FrontierCode 2.0 (41.3). On ExploitBench it scored 80, still behind Claude Opus 5.5 (91) and GPT-6.2 Sol (100), but it lands within two points of both on CWE-Bench. An Ultrafast variant runs 10x faster, and there is a dedicated SWE-2.1 Cyber edition; Pro, Max and Teams users get it free until December 8.

### [Claude artifacts can now be installed as Mac and Windows apps](https://claude.com/resources/articles/install-as-app)
*(3 newsletters)*

In beta on all plans, an installed artifact gets its own icon and window, can call Claude and connectors, updates automatically when the artifact is edited, and can be shared. TLDR Founders pitches it as a low-effort way for knowledge workers to build and maintain internal tools without a deployment pipeline.

### [Claude Ship automates PR review, fixes and approvals](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-claude-ship)
*(3 newsletters)*

Anthropic's new Claude Ship bundles Code Review, PR Steward and Claude Approvals on GitHub, running against team-defined policies and spend limits so routine fixes can be reviewed and approved without a human babysitting each PR. The Code links Addy Osmani's [cookbook on the /ship command](https://claude.dev/blog/the-work-after-you-open-a-pr/) for wiring CI fixes and review follow-ups into it.

### [FCC approves SpaceX's million-satellite orbital data center plan](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/fcc-approves-spacex-plan-to-put-ai-data-centers-in-space/)
*(2 newsletters)*

The FCC gave conditional approval for up to 1 million satellites for orbital AI compute, overriding objections from Amazon, Viasat and astronomers. Anthropic and Google have reportedly already committed billions to rent capacity. SpaceX has until 2033 to launch the first satellite and 2040 for the full constellation, with launch cost and in-space cooling still unsolved.

### [Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia CEO, and the fallout is just starting](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/23/2026/jensen-huang-reinstated-as-nvidia-ceo)
*(2 newsletters)*

Huang's reinstatement, with lobbying from the President, added roughly $1T to Nvidia's market cap, and congressional hearings begin December 3. U.S. officials reportedly lack proof that Alice Huang was tasked with extracting secrets, but her former firm Megaspeed was blacklisted Monday over suspected chip smuggling to China. Zvi's [long read](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/jensen-huang-reinstated-as-nvidia) argues Nvidia will now return to maximizing China sales.

### [The harness matters more than the model](https://simple.ai/p/same-models-different-results-the-harness-is-the-difference)
*(2 newsletters)*

Dharmesh Shah argues the software around a model (context, tools, skills, guardrails) drives most results, and that purpose-built harnesses beat general ones because they own the context. At QCon SF, Mad Labs' Manju Rajashekhar made the same case, [mapping harness components onto operating system functions](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/harness-new-operating-system/). Useful framing for anyone pitching agent work to clients.

### [OpenAI and Cisco open-source Shirudo to shield unpatched Linux systems](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/t3iuFZ)
*(2 newsletters)*

Shirudo uses eBPF to block vulnerable code paths on running Linux systems with no patch or restart, buying security teams time to ship permanent fixes. It is BSD 2-Clause licensed, and it lands the same week as the agentic-attack warnings above.

### [MCP servers can now ship skills, run long tasks and ask for approval in Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/skills-over-mcp-tasks-and-more)
*(2 newsletters)*

Skills delivered over MCP are in public beta (on by default for Pro, Max and Ultra; admin-enabled for Team), while Tasks for long-running background work and Elicitation for mid-task user input are now generally available in Claude and Claude Code. In the same vein, [Google Workspace made MCP servers and its CLI generally available](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/mcp-servers-cli-standardized-usage-tiers-now-available.html), and agents must move off direct REST APIs to MCP by February 1, 2027.

### [GPT-6 models can design working proteins with reasoning and code alone](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI says GPT-6 class models matched or beat biology-specific models at protein design without calling specialist tools, and lab tests with a partner confirmed some designs worked. Screening candidates still relied on specialist tooling.
