## Top Stories

### [Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum in an all-stock mega-deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/in-one-of-the-largest-deals-in-tech-history-marvell-buys-lumentum-for-115b/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Marvell, which designs custom AI chips and data center interconnect, is buying optical networking maker Lumentum at 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share. TechCrunch, Rundown, and The CEO Report put it at $115B, larger than Microsoft's $68.7B Activision deal, while the WSJ headline cited by TLDR says $80B, so the figure depends on how the stock is valued. Nvidia is a major shareholder in both companies, and the deal is not expected to close until the first half of 2028.

### [Anthropic's updated S-1 says it may be close to recursive self-improvement](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-warns-that-its-very-close-to-rsi-capability-aka-self-building-ai/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Anthropic's amended IPO prospectus reports nearly $17B in Q3 revenue and a $567M operating loss, and adds language that upcoming models could "imminently" cross its internal threshold for AI-driven AI progress. One [analysis of the S-1](https://www.tilomitra.com/blog/anthropic-s1-in-charts) argues the real risk is years of locked-in compute commitments paid for by customers who can cut back any month, on top of copyright suits and heavy reliance on the big clouds.

### [Critical Linux TCP flaw (CVE-2026-98432) lets attackers get root without a password](https://atlasum.net/blog/cve-2026-98432-linux-ipv4-tcp-uaf)
*(2 newsletters)*

A CVSS 9.8 use-after-free in the Linux IPv4 TCP handshake, which Anthropic had Claude turn into a working exploit, fires before the application even sees the connection. It targets servers with a multipath TCP listener, which Go programs open by default since Go 1.24. Debian, SUSE, Oracle, and Amazon Linux have shipped fixed kernels; Ubuntu and RHEL had not at press time, so check [the remediation steps](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/linux-kernel-tcp-flaw-with-98-cvss.html).

### [GPT-6 Astra improves other models by editing their weights directly](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)
*(2 newsletters)*

MIT researchers showed Astra lifting a small Qwen model's math score from 7.9% to 48.1% by changing just 0.016% of its weights to fix a repetition habit, with no retraining. The same trick barely moved Google's Gemma 3, which had no single flaw to fix, and a [counterpoint](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights/) argues normal training is still the better way to update weights. A separate post on [unlocking latent skills in Google's model](https://dejan.ai/blog/latent-skills/) points in the same direction.

### [Karpathy builds HackerTube in about two hours with Claude Code](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/)
*(2 newsletters)*

HackerTube surfaces 6,454 videos across 150 topics that the Hacker News community keeps recommending, mined from 43M comments. Opus 5.5 handled the pipeline and deployment while Sonnet 5.5 labeled comments (about 504M input tokens, mostly cached); Karpathy supplied only an API key, server, and domain. Personalization stays in local browser state with no accounts.

### [Our organizations are being DDoSed from the inside](https://karrisaarinen.com/posts/our-organizations-are-being-ddosed-from-the-inside/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Karri Saarinen argues that as teams and AI agents generate more output, they all draw on the same limited capacity to review, understand, and decide. Output is not proof of value, and a working implementation only proves something can be built, not that it should be. The fix is clarifying intent upfront and deliberate release gating.

### [You're probably overprompting Claude](https://archive.codenewsletter.ai/2115688494865465370)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic's cookbook says newer Claude models need far less instruction: set a clear goal with a verifier, trim rules written for older models, and be selective about subagents. Claude Code itself removed over 80% of its system prompt for newer models with no measurable drop on coding evals.

### [Cloudflare services for private origins is generally available](https://blog.cloudflare.com/private-origins-public-to-private/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Cloudflare can now route public traffic to private origin IPs over existing Cloudflare One connectivity, applying WAF, caching, bot management, and Workers without a public IP, open firewall port, or cloudflared on the origin. Argo Smart Routing and Dedicated CDN Egress IPs are not supported, and health checks require Load Balancing.
