## Top Stories

### [Anthropic launches Claude Ship to take PRs from open to merge](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-claude-ship)
*(5 newsletters)*

Claude Ship is a set of agents for GitHub pull requests: Code Review finds and verifies bugs, PR Steward fixes failing CI and merge conflicts (noting how to undo each fix), and Claude Approvals signs off on routine changes against a policy file in the repo, looping in a human when required. It is available on Team and Enterprise plans for GitHub repositories, with a new `/ship` command in Claude Code; some pieces are still in beta and usage can add cost.

### [OpenAI and Cisco open-source Shirudo to shield Linux servers mid-patch](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/)
*(4 newsletters)*

Shirudo is a free Linux runtime that deploys temporary eBPF "shields" to block exploitable behavior while teams test and roll out permanent patches, without restarting services. AI agents can write the shields, operators can observe their effect on live workloads, and every shield needs human approval before going live.

### [Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia CEO, and tells staff he is in daily therapy](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)
*(3 newsletters)*

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Huang as president, CEO, and director less than two weeks after his resignation, after US officials confirmed no national security risk. At an [all-hands the next day](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-24/nvidia-s-huang-to-host-all-hands-meeting-a-day-after-his-return), he acknowledged violating the code of conduct and said he is in daily therapy. Nvidia is adding a National Security and Export Control Committee and a Chief Compliance Officer, while independent directors investigate claims he was targeted by foreign intelligence.

### [xAI founders launch Epochal, a "time machine" to teach AI to forecast](https://epochal.com/blog/introducing-epochal)
*(3 newsletters)*

Three xAI founding members (Guodong Zhang reportedly put in $100M of his own money) launched Epochal and its first product, Time Portal: a 1T+ page archive that lets agents browse the web as it existed at a given date or even hour, with the agent's clock and OS rolled back too. In testing, agents forecast 347 Kalshi and Polymarket questions live, and replays weeks later made nearly the same calls. There is a [public visualizer](https://visualizer.epochal.com/).

### [Meta brings its Muse assistant into Instagram DMs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/meta-brings-its-muse-ai-assistant-directly-into-instagram-dms/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Instagram users can now prompt Muse directly in messages to ask about a post, place, or product, or hand it tasks like turning a recipe reel into a grocery list or saved travel videos into an itinerary. Zuckerberg says shopping is Muse's top use case, with an average purchase size around $200.

### [Z.ai's GLM-5.4 is its first "Loop Transformer" flagship](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4)
*(3 newsletters)*

The 750B-parameter model runs the same 28-layer block twice, getting more compute per token without extra parameters, and Z.ai claims performance comparable to 2T to 3T parameter models. Demos go beyond code into self-narrated videos, playable games, CAD, and robotics. It is live on the GLM Coding Plan (from $18/month), with API and open weights coming; Z.ai admits it can still overthink.

### [The agent-shopper economy arrives just in time for Black Friday](https://www.a16z.news/p/welcome-to-the-b2a-economy-how-agents)
*(2 newsletters)*

a16z says we are entering a "business to agent" economy and, with The Prompting Company, ran 11,000+ agent shopping tests: stores with better return terms won 86% of equal-price comparisons, and every checkout requiring a new account stopped the agent. Shopify meanwhile [turned on agentic checkout for ChatGPT](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/manage-whether-ai-agents-can-buy-your-products-for-shoppers-through-chatgpt) by default for eligible stores. Amazon and Cloudflare are blocking shopping agents, Walmart is welcoming them, and Adobe projects AI-driven holiday traffic up 130%.

### [CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler for $43.9B, the largest security deal ever](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-acquire-zscaler-bringing-network-security-and-zero)
*(2 newsletters)*

The all-stock deal combines endpoint protection with Zscaler's zero-trust network and cloud security, creating a company with more than $10B in ARR. CrowdStrike targets $500M in annual cost cuts and about $1B in added revenue; Zscaler CEO Jay Chaudhry keeps running the business and joins CrowdStrike's board. It tops Google's $32B Wiz buy and Cisco's $28B Splunk deal.

### [Microsoft's outage report reveals testing gaps; company declares "Code Red"](https://www.theregister.com/saas/2026/11/23/microsoft-mega-outage-report-reveals-testing-gaps-and-emails-that-never-arrived/5311656)

### [Tidal will delete fully AI-generated music starting December 9](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/1041362/tidal-ai-music-ban)
*(2 newsletters)*

Tidal becomes the first subscription streamer to ban, not just label, fully AI-made tracks: it will remove roughly 29M existing songs and expects to reject 80,000 to 85,000 uploads a day. AI tracks were 43% of submissions since late August, yet about 96% of them reportedly get zero plays. AI-assisted music is still allowed.

### [Amazon pays $7.5B for Decart's AI optimization tech and team](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Amazon is acquiring the Decart Optimization Stack plus its founders and researchers. The software keeps AI workloads efficient across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs, and Trainium, which matters as Amazon prepares to sell Trainium chips more broadly.

### [AI keeps knocking down famous math problems](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/riemann-hypothesis-anthropic-math-levent-alpoge-75351bcb)
*(2 newsletters)*

The WSJ profiles Anthropic's Levent Alpoge, whose AI-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis (17 million lines, formally certified) has been verified by two independent groups. Separately, Anthropic [said](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/main/vp-vnp) an internal Claude model solved a 47-year-old problem tied to P vs NP, not yet checked. Scott Aaronson [writes](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10362) that the formal proofs hold up technically and some mathematicians are rethinking their careers.
