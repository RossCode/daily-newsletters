## Top Stories

### [Anthropic raises $81B in the largest IPO ever](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-ant-live-updates.html)
*(4 newsletters)*

Anthropic began trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT after pricing at $83, above its $70 to $78 target range, raising about $81.3B and topping SpaceX's record from five months ago. Shares opened at $111.50, briefly valuing the company near $2T, before closing the first day at $97.17, up roughly 17% even as all three major indexes fell; retail demand was reportedly the largest of any IPO in history. The raise leaves Anthropic with roughly $220B in cash, 96% of its 5K+ employees are now paper millionaires, and Amazon's and Google's early stakes are worth $161B and $147B. Dario Amodei used the opening bell to pledge that Anthropic "will not bend to either business nor political pressure" on model safety, a promise every earnings call will now test.

### [Apple is building an autonomous AI agent for devices, apps, browsing and coding](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/apple-is-working-on-ai-agent-for-device-control-apps-browsing-and-coding)
*(2 newsletters)*

Apple is hiring a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agentic assistant that can write code, browse the web, control apps and coordinate specialized subagents across multi-step tasks, with a launch eyed for as soon as late 2027. The move follows an App Store increasingly dominated by third-party agents; macOS 27.3 will add a warning prompting users to reconsider granting those agents control of their Macs.

### [PostgreSQL 19 released](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/postgresql-19-released-3449/)
*(2 newsletters)*

The headline feature is `REPACK (CONCURRENTLY)`, which reclaims table space while reads and writes continue. Applications can now wait for a standby to replay a specific write, and an insert conflict can return the existing row directly, which simplifies get-or-create queries; a new pg_plan_advice extension gives more control over plan stability.

### [Meta's Muse agent reportedly exposed other users' private data](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-mixing-up-conversations-and-exposing-private-data-report-says/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Meta employees told Reuters that Muse has been mixing up conversations across accounts, surfacing other people's childcare requests, orders and bank balances; one tester got an unprompted Capital One and Schwab balance update in a chat about tide times. Meta says staff test different builds than the public, but Muse already has 10M+ downloads.

### [Alljoined's AIR-O turns brainwaves into real-time images](https://x.com/alljoined/status/2122736523007447048)
*(2 newsletters)*

Startup Alljoined unveiled AIR-O, a model that reads noninvasive brain signals and generates an image of whatever object a person is focusing on, updating as their focus shifts. The company claims it adapts to new users in 15 minutes, though the demo has not been independently verified.

### [Google picks Blue Origin's New Glenn for Project Suncatcher](https://www.blueorigin.com/news/google-selects-new-glenn-for-project-suncatcher)
*(2 newsletters)*

New Glenn will launch Google's solar-powered data center satellites into a Sun-synchronous orbit in 2029, where they get near-continuous solar power. Each satellite carries roughly a data-center rack's worth of compute on Google's own AI chips, though [Ars Technica notes](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/google-turns-to-blue-origin-for-a-rocket-but-its-long-term-problem-remains/) the program's long-term launch-cost problem remains.
