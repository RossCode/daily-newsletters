## Top Stories

### [Anthropic launches Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)
*(4 newsletters)*

Anthropic rolled out three new artifact types to paid plans: Sheets (shareable, multi-tab spreadsheets with formulas), Whiteboards (editable visual boards), and Data, the renamed and now generally available Dashboards, which answers questions over warehouses like Redshift, BigQuery, and ClickHouse. In beta, data teams can certify the queries everyone should use, and live sharing shows current numbers to colleagues without their own connector access. Interactive connectors now embed apps like Adobe and Atlassian directly in Claude, Salesforce, NetSuite, and Workday plugins ship in beta, and Claude Meetings is coming soon. Superhuman framed it as a direct run at Excel and Google Sheets.

### [OpenAI opens ChatGPT to businesses with profiles and Business Agents](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)
*(4 newsletters)*

Companies can now claim and verify a profile through ChatGPT's Business Hub, update details and product feeds, and see mentions, views, and clicks. Eligible US e-commerce and online-service businesses can also launch Business Agents that answer customer questions inside ChatGPT, which can double as landing pages for ChatGPT ads. It is in beta and widely read as a challenge to Google Business Profiles; The Rundown published a [step-by-step setup guide](https://app.therundown.ai/guides/claim-and-set-up-your-business-on-chatgpt). Separately, ChatGPT visual ads are starting to appear during image generation.

### [Nvidia's board moves toward replacing Jensen Huang](https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad)
*(2 newsletters)*

The WSJ reports Nvidia's directors are preparing to remove Huang as CEO, and Bloomberg says the company is working with an outside firm to identify candidates and may name an interim chief. The move came hours after the NYT reported a second affair, this one with a vice president who reports to him, while The Economist and members of Congress have called for his resignation. Nvidia shed more than $427B in market value over two sessions and fell about 8% after hours as the latest reports landed.

### [Gemini 4 Carbon is generally available](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Google's strongest model is now open to all paying API users and enterprises, and rolling out to AI Ultra subscribers in Antigravity, the Gemini app, and AI Mode in Search. Google claims first place on CursorBench 4.0, WANDR, and AutomationBench at more than 2x lower cost per task than the next best external model, priced at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens. The release also previews [/swarm](https://antigravity.google/docs/swarm/), a multi-agent mode in Antigravity where named agents split work and coordinate on a shared message board.

### [Anthropic's IPO is reportedly multiple times oversubscribed](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic launched its roadshow, planning to offer 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Bloomberg reports orders already exceed the shares on offer several times over, which would make it larger than SpaceX's debut and the biggest stock listing ever.

### [United Airlines blocks AI agents from booking flights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/bots-dont-fly-here-united-airlines-restricts-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-203943442.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

United is restricting unauthorized third-party AI agents from booking on its platforms and outside sites ahead of holiday travel, citing accidental purchases, exposure of sensitive data, and FAA/DOT rules that need human sign-off. CEO Scott Kirby was blunter, telling the WSJ he doesn't want "another middleman." It lands the same day as a16z's piece on the [B2A economy](https://www.a16z.news/p/welcome-to-the-b2a-economy) arguing businesses should treat agents as first-class customers.

### [OpenAI acquires AI travel startup Odessia](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-acquires-odessia-travel-startup-created-by-sonder-founder/)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI bought Odessia, a year-old AI travel agent from Sonder founder Francis Davidson, in what looks like an acqui-hire. The product, launched about five months ago on a $6M Sequoia-led pre-seed, has already been shut down, and Davidson now works at OpenAI on travel.

### [The era of fully autonomous companies](https://www.generalintelligencecompany.com/writing/the-era-of-fully-autonomous-companies)
*(2 newsletters)*

This essay argues companies run entirely by agents, with humans only at the bottom of the hierarchy for specific tasks, are arriving now, and predicts millions of them (many profitable) within a year. It lays out what they need: persistent shared state, coordination, payment access, and goals checkable against real business results, while flagging demand generation, bot defenses, and identity requirements as the hard obstacles.

### [DeepMind's AlphaProof Code verifies Signal's cryptography](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)
*(2 newsletters)*

AlphaProof Code pairs human-reviewed specifications with AI agents that generate Lean proofs, and verified key security properties of Signal's compiled cryptographic code in four weeks using more than six million lines of Lean. As a sanity check, it also proved two deliberately reintroduced historical bugs insecure.
