## Top Stories

### [Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)
*(8 newsletters)*

Anthropic shipped Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, which share one underlying model: Fable is broadly available with stronger cyber and bio guardrails, while Mythos is reserved for vetted security and life sciences teams. Pricing drops to $8/$40 per million tokens (about 20% cheaper than 5.1, though still double Opus 5.5), output is up to 30% faster, and Fable debuted at No. 1 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index, edging out Google's Gemini 4 Carbon. Ethan Mollick called it ["almost" AGI](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/fable-52-and-jagged-agi), a 1,000-agent team [formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 28.7 hours](https://claude.dev/blog/safely-scaling-multi-agent-teams-with-fable-5-2/), and Anthropic framed the release as "pacing, not pausing." Its own [model-selection guide](https://claude.dev/blog/which-claude-model-should-you-build-with/) still recommends starting most work on Opus 5.5 and saving Fable for the hardest technical tasks.

### [Jensen Huang spy scandal deepens at Nvidia](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jensen-huangs-yearslong-ties-alice-huang-enabled-nvidia-chip-sales-china)
*(6 newsletters)*

Huang admitted an affair with Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive US intelligence believes was recruited by Chinese intelligence, and Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation. The Information now reports the relationship helped move $7.5B+ in Nvidia chips into China; Nvidia fell 4.27% Monday (about $190B in market cap), a securities class action landed minutes after close, and Trump confirmed an FBI probe. Congress gave Huang until Wednesday to schedule a closed-door briefing, and Democrats gain subpoena power in January. ([WSJ timeline](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/a-timeline-of-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-suspected-spy-drama-05cbebf3), [TechCrunch on the lawsuits](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/the-race-is-on-to-sue-nvidia/))

### [Google unveils TPU 8tx and 8i Max to press Nvidia](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/tpu-8i-max-and-8tx/)
*(6 newsletters)*

Google announced two specialized eighth-gen TPU systems shipping in 2027: the 8tx pairs Google silicon with AMD processors and keeps data encrypted even during inference for governments and regulated industries, while the 8i Max fuses two chips for long-context work like holding an entire codebase in view. Google has nearly 100 TPU customers including Anthropic and Apple, with all available supply spoken for, and Bloomberg Intelligence projects TPU sales rising from $12B this year to $135B+ by 2028. For developers, TorchTPU now runs PyTorch and Hugging Face models natively. The timing, the day after Huang's apology, was widely noted.

### [Anthropic sets IPO terms at up to $1.3 trillion](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-ipo-valuation-8337b669)
*(5 newsletters)*

Anthropic plans to sell 825 million shares at $70 to $78 and raise another $26 billion from investors including Google and AMD, with trading expected to begin Thursday of next week. The range is below the roughly $1.5T some investors expected, after pushback amid AI-sector jitters. An updated filing says Anthropic now considers its AI R&D capability threshold met, and [Ramp data](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/ramp-data-shows-ai-spending-stalling-just-as-anthropic-nears-its-ipo/) shows enterprise token spend flattening even as usage climbs. Separately, Anthropic [bought $3.4B in gas generators](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-joins-ais-natural-gas-frenzy-buys-3-4b-worth-of-turbines/) (1.7 GW) to work around grid queues.

### [AI R&D acceleration pulls timelines into 2027](https://blog.aifutures.org/p/q35-timelines-update-looks-like-the)
*(5 newsletters)*

Following METR's estimate that Anthropic now improves about 2x as fast largely thanks to AI, the AI Futures Project (authors of AI 2027) moved its forecast for a superhuman AI researcher back into 2027, with Daniel Kokotajlo at 50/50 by May 2027 and the group saying a pause "should be the top priority." Zvi Mowshowitz [argues](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-r-and-d-is-accelerating-towards) full recursive self-improvement may be under a year away, while a separate thread notes [OpenAI's internal data](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/2119541238856663473.html) shows models still struggle with its R&D work.

### [Meta's Muse Ember undercuts Haiku and Luna on agent costs](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember)
*(4 newsletters)*

Muse Ember is Meta's smallest model, built for high-volume agent steps like tool calls, routing, extraction, and small coding jobs. It tied Haiku 5.5 at 36 on Artificial Analysis' index (GPT-6 Luna scored 28) and averaged $0.14 per test task versus $0.41 for Haiku by using fewer tokens. Meta pitches it as the worker paired with Muse Wonder as planner, and is reportedly building an enterprise sales push through consulting partners.

### [Anthropic stretches Claude across the office](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic launched Claude Sheets and Whiteboards in beta, made Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) generally available, and announced Claude Meetings, which joins video calls as a visible participant and drafts plans while people talk (Google Meet beta for Team and Enterprise). Upgraded connectors now open tools like Adobe and Atlassian fully inside Claude, part of a broader race with OpenAI to replace Microsoft and Google office suites.

### [ChatGPT opens for business with verified brand profiles](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI's Businesses beta lets companies claim a verified profile, see how often ChatGPT mentions them, and deploy shopping agents that paid ads can point to. It is essentially Google Business Profile plus analytics for AI answers, giving brands direct visibility into what had been guesswork for AEO.

### [Amazon's Ring launches a flying indoor security drone](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/amazons-ring-unveils-flying-drone-robot-to-patrol-homes-businesses.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

The $250 Ring Drone Cam flies room to room for up to eight minutes when an alarm trips or on a schedule, shipping January 27 pending FCC approval. Businesses get the $1,000 Ring Rover Cam, a wheeled robot with a periscope camera, shipping December 16. Expect privacy pushback.

### [Uber turns SpotHero into in-app parking](https://www.uber.com/us/en/newsroom/uber-parking-has-arrived/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Uber Parking is live in 15 US cities with access to 13,000+ SpotHero locations, and Uber One members get 10% back. The longer play is using those lots as staging areas for robotaxi partners to cut deadhead miles.

### [OpenAI raises $11B at a $1.25T valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)
*(2 newsletters)*

The round could reach $20B but falls well short of the earlier $30B target, a sign of investor caution on AI valuations. OpenAI also [acquired Pierre Computer Company](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4245719/openai-buys-tiny-software-developer-to-improve-codex.html) to bring its Git infrastructure into Codex.

### [Apple pushes to make Siri a proactive agent by 2027](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/08/apple-working-on-agentic-siri-ai-next-year-report/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Per Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, CEO John Ternus wants Siri to track goals, plan next steps, and act without being prompted, likely via App Intents, to compete with Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, and Claude Duet. Apple has not committed to a timeline.
