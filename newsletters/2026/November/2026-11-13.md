## Top Stories

### [Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)
*(5 newsletters)*

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and board member effective immediately, days after admitting to an affair with a woman US intelligence believes was a Chinese asset; reports of a second relationship with an Nvidia executive (NYT) and a third with another employee (Reuters) followed. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board weighs internal and external candidates, and long-time director Harvey Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair. Nvidia has shed more than $855B in market value since Friday, earnings land November 17, House committee chairs want a closed-door briefing, and Trump has publicly [floated Tim Cook](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/trump-floats-apple-chairman-tim-cook-as-jensen-huangs-successor/) as successor. Huang had famously said he did not believe in succession planning.

### [Google ships Gemini Decision 1 and the Decisions API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)
*(5 newsletters)*

Gemini Decision 1 scores bounded choices (yes/no, category, rubric) instead of generating text, evaluating up to 1,000 questions against one shared input of text, images, audio, video, or PDFs in a single pass. Google cites 150 to 250 ms latency and $0.10 per million input tokens with no output charge, so your code acts directly on the returned probabilities. Target uses are model routing, policy checks, triage, and rubric scoring; newsletters note that confidence scores are not correctness guarantees.

### [Anthropic launches Claude Meetings, Sheets, Whiteboards, and interactive connectors](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)
*(3 newsletters)*

Claude Meetings joins Google Meet calls to take notes, pull information, and start work on tasks while people are still talking (beta for Team and Enterprise). Claude Sheets builds spreadsheets with live formulas and Whiteboards gives teams a shared board, both in beta on paid plans, while Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) is now GA. New interactive connectors let users work inside Adobe, Notion, Atlassian, Salesforce, and HubSpot from the Claude interface, and Computerworld frames the push as direct competition with Microsoft and Google for workplace collaboration.

### [Grok Bot 1.0 becomes a team colleague](https://flaviocopes.com/grok-bot-1-0/)
*(3 newsletters)*

SpaceXAI's Grok Bot 1.0 adds publishable Team Bots, Slack and Microsoft Teams integration, attendance in Meet and Zoom calls, shared connectors, and reviewed learnings that persist across the organization. The pitch has shifted from an assistant you hand tasks to toward a proactive colleague that takes on work unprompted and remembers team preferences. Paired with Claude Meetings the same week, agents joining meetings is now a mainstream feature category.

### [Google opens pre-orders for Gemini smart glasses](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/xr-ar/google-intelligent-eyewear-collections-pre-order/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Built with Samsung and sold through Warby Parker and Gentle Monster frames starting at $395, the 41-gram glasses have a 12MP camera, open-ear audio, nine-hour battery, and no in-lens display. Google is leaning on privacy: bystander faces are redacted before Gemini sees them, and recording stops if the indicator light is covered. They ship December 10 in the US and South Korea, work with iPhones, and take aim at Meta, which shipped 81% of AI glasses in the first half of the year.

### [Massive Microsoft 365 outage hits Outlook, Teams, and Copilot](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-outlook-aws-google.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

Outlook, Teams, and Microsoft 365 went down shortly before 1 p.m. ET Thursday, with thousands of North American users affected. Microsoft blamed elevated load and servers unexpectedly becoming unavailable, which it restarted. AWS and Google users also reported problems on Downdetector the same afternoon.

### [Gemini 4 Hydrogen lands in Search and the API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Google's new fast, low-cost model has a 1M-token context window and reportedly outperforms Gemini 4 Argon at its highest thinking level while running 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. It now powers AI Mode in Search, which Google calls its biggest model upgrade to Search yet, with better handling of messy multi-part questions plus more images and comparison tables.

### [Blue launches an agent-first phone](https://heyblue.com/)
*(2 newsletters)*

YC-backed Blue, co-founded by an ex-Google and Scale AI product manager, runs a custom Android build (Blue OS) on Pixel 10 Pro hardware that lets agents work across apps on your behalf, priced at $1,299 in public beta. The developer takeaway from The Code: if agents start tapping through your UI instead of users, app design needs to become agent-friendly.

### [AWS Emulator gives coding agents a local cloud](https://github.com/aws/aws-emulator)
*(2 newsletters)*

AWS shipped a local stand-in for more than 200 services so developers and agents can run test loops offline in development and CI without an AWS account or cloud costs. Autonomous agents reportedly wrote the whole thing, though it cannot yet simulate throttling, outages, or regional differences.
