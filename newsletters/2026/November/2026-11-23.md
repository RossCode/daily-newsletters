## Top Stories

### [Anthropic mathematician and Claude claim a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Anthropic's Levent Alpöge says he and Claude cracked the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis after about six weeks of work, delivering the proof as roughly 17M lines of machine-checkable Lean. The Lean community [certified it on Saturday](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/) with "full confidence," even though no human fully understands it yet, and the Association for Human Mathematics dismissed it as AI publicity. A competing human-only proof from Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo surfaced hours later, though others quickly flagged problems in it.

### [Waymo pauses freeway rides nationwide after California DMV order](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/waymo-suspends-freeway-rides-nationwide-after-california-dmv-order/)
*(2 newsletters)*

California's DMV restricted Waymo from state freeways after three robotaxis drove into an active Route 101 construction closure and a separate incident involving Highway Patrol vehicles, calling driverless freeway rides an "imminent hazard." Waymo complied and paused freeway rides in all 15 US cities it serves, not just California.

### [Gemini can now operate your Mac](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)
*(2 newsletters)*
