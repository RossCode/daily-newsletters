## Top Stories

### [Nvidia without Jensen Huang: resignation, petition and a possible comeback](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-jensen-huang-return-tech.html)
*(11 newsletters)*

Huang resigned after admitting an affair with Alice Huang, whom US officials believe is a Chinese spy. Further affairs were then reported, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. A petition and open letter to reinstate him has passed 160K signatures, with Musk, Travis Kalanick and Garry Tan among the signers and 7,000+ signers claiming to be Nvidia staff. The FT reports Huang is privately weighing a return, and Trump says he "would not mind" one, but CNBC's source says the board is not considering it and is focused on national-security exposure while it hires a search firm. Semafor reports three DOJ probes touching Nvidia, Megaspeed and Supermicro, and Stratechery argues the exit could puncture the AI trade. Nvidia's Q3 results are still set for today, November 17. [Nvidia announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition), [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/obituaries/)

### [Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/)
*(7 newsletters)*

Hydrogen replaces the Flash line. Google says it finishes jobs 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, triples the Terminal-Bench 4.1 score and has a 1M-token context, at $1.50/$7.50 per million input/output tokens. It scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis index, and a new Antigravity Orchestrator pairs it with Carbon for near-Carbon results on 24% less quota. It is also now the model behind Google Search.

### [Anthropic ships Claude Meetings and self-hosted Managed Agents](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings)
*(5 newsletters)*

Claude Meetings, in beta for Team and Enterprise, puts Claude into Google Meet as a visible participant. It answers questions, drafts documents and whiteboards live, and captures action items, with integrations planned for Granola, Gong, Otter and others. A separate private beta lets companies run the whole Managed Agents stack in their own cloud or on-prem with zero data retention. Superhuman notes that Anthropic shares start trading this Thursday. [Self-hosted agents](https://claude.com/resources/articles/self-hosted-claude-managed-agents)

### [ChatGPT Wallet lets agents check out for you](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)
*(5 newsletters)*

OpenAI built Wallet with Amex, Mastercard and Visa. It tokenizes your card for each purchase an agent makes and requires Face ID or a fingerprint for every one. It is rolling out first on Dots to US Pro users. Replit Agent can now buy business supplies and run Google Ads too, while United and Amazon are moving the other way and blocking outside shopping agents that skip high-margin add-ons. [Replit](https://replit.com/blog/replit-agent-ads-commerce), [Tunguz on bot blocking](https://tomtunguz.com/no-shirt-no-shoes-no-service-for-bots/)

### [Microsoft 365 outage runs from Thursday into Saturday](https://www.theregister.com/saas/2026/11/13/microsoft-services-restored-to-all-customers-company-says-its-deeply-sorry/5309609)
*(5 newsletters)*

Exchange Online, Outlook, Copilot, SharePoint, Intune and parts of Teams were down for roughly 36 hours across 74 countries. US reports peaked near 130K, and the OpenAI and Anthropic 365 connectors broke too. Microsoft blames failing server health checks and a network capacity change. Commentary has focused on the cost of single-vendor dependence, and some skeptics speculate about AI-written code, which Microsoft denies. [The Register on dependence](https://www.theregister.com/saas/2026/11/13/microsoft-outage-gives-customers-a-crash-course-in-dependence-on-redmond/5309805)

[Blackwell forensics](https://unemploymentlabs.com/checkpoint-forensics)

### [Anthropic's guide to building always-on agents](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-always-on-agents)
*(4 newsletters)*

The guide is built around a core loop plus five primitives: an event inbox, durable timers, persistent memory, a permission system and an outbox for outbound actions. It was picked up widely as the reference pattern for long-running agents.

### [Fireworks AI breach spreads to Uber and Cursor](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/uber-confirms-hackers-stole-thousands-of-drivers-license-information-in-data-breach/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Uber confirmed that driver's license data was stolen through October's Fireworks AI vendor breach, with thousands affected across three states so far, and Notion AI chats leaked in the same hack. Cursor linked the breach to a hijacked Claude Code extension: versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 were live in its marketplace for about 11 hours on Oct 29 and tried to plant SSH backdoors on nearly 5,000 machines. Affected users should check for unauthorized SSH keys and rotate credentials.

### [Critical Apache HTTP Server RCE (CVE-2026-119618)](https://www.wiz.io/blog/cve-2026-119618-rce-apache-http-server-vulnerability)
*(2 newsletters)*

A race condition in mod_http2's `assign_headers()` causes heap corruption that can lead to RCE. Red Hat scores it 9.8, it affects versions 2.4.55 through 2.4.70, and Wiz says 64% of cloud environments have vulnerable resources. Upgrade to 2.4.71 or disable HTTP/2. A public PoC shows the corruption, but no working RCE exploit has been released.
