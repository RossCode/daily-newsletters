## Top Stories

### [A week of outages: Microsoft, the FAA, Chase, and now Apple](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/apple-microsoft-faa-jpmorgan-outages.html)
*(4 newsletters)*

Over four days, Microsoft 365 went down worldwide, an FAA equipment fault forced a nationwide ground stop, a Chase software bug in "an on-premises hardware system" rippled payment delays across other banks, and Apple's iCloud sign-in, App Store, and Apple Music failed for all users. The failures appear unrelated, and the Director of National Intelligence dismissed suspicions of malicious activity. Microsoft [traced its outage](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/16/microsoft-global-outage-cause) to a months-old bug plus faulty network connections and reduced capacity, while security researchers point to a broader pattern: AI is accelerating vulnerability discovery, and companies are struggling to ship the resulting flood of patches without breaking things.

### [Jensen Huang apologizes and says he would return as Nvidia CEO](https://www.wsj.com/tech/jensen-huang-statement-nvidia-ceo-9dedca36)
*(4 newsletters)*

In his first statement since resigning, Huang apologized for "serious mistakes" and said he would be honored to return if directors asked, with a petition passing 188K signatures and President Trump voicing support. Board chair Harvey Jones says the company does not intend to reinstate him and is focused on the national security probe into whether Alice Huang helped divert Nvidia chips to China, though [some directors are still pushing](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/17/2026/some-nvidia-directors-push-to-bring-huang-back-despite-board-opposition) for his return. Acting CEO Colette Kress led Tuesday's earnings call, and a viral thread on Blackwell chips reaching China through Southeast Asian data centers is keeping the export story hot.

### [OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol to rough reviews](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI's new professional-work pair is rolling out in ChatGPT, Codex, and the API: Astra climbs to second on Artificial Analysis behind Fable 5.2, and the cheaper Sol matches September's GPT-6 Astra at roughly 15% of the cost per task. But Astra shows no real coding gain, Sol regresses on coding versus GPT-6.1, and several prominent testers panned the release; OpenAI also rated Astra at its "High" self-improvement threshold, a first for a public model. The timing is awkward, landing days before Anthropic's IPO, while TLDR Founders notes [Fable 5.2 already cut frontier pricing by 20%](https://x.com/jaminball/status/2121310809842479320).

### [ULA's Vulcan fails, destroying 40 Amazon Leo satellites](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/boeing-lockheed-rocket-amazon-satellites.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

Vulcan failed about 10 minutes into Sunday's flight, the first total mission failure in ULA's 20-year history and the first of Amazon's 38 booked launches. Ars Technica points to the core booster's Blue Origin BE-4 engines firing past cutoff as the rocket began to spin. Amazon has about 400 of a needed 3,200+ satellites in orbit, so the bigger loss is time against Starlink; its next launch is an Ariane 6 no earlier than November 23.

### [Uber and Zipline launch drone food delivery in Texas](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/uber-and-ziplines-drone-delivery-service-takes-off-in-texas-with-dozens-of-cities-to-follow/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Uber Eats customers in Spring, Texas can now pick a drone option for participating restaurants, with orders up to eight pounds lowered by tether from drones flying around 300 feet at up to 70 mph. Dallas is next, and the partners target dozens more U.S. cities and a million deliveries a day by the end of 2029.

### [Liquid AI's d1.2 decision models top the Decision Index](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/d1-2)
*(2 newsletters)*

d1.2 and d1.2-ultra are omnimodal decision models that take text, images, and audio and return structured answers with confidence scores, saying "not found" rather than inventing missing values. That makes them useful for auto-approving confident calls and routing uncertain ones to humans. Ultra ranks first on Artificial Analysis's Decision Index v2.1, with pricing from $0.02 per million input tokens.

### [Tencent's Hy4 flash rivals Claude Haiku at a fraction of the cost](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-flash-to-power-high-speed-cost-efficient-ai-deployment-for-everyday-workflows/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Tencent's new low-cost model came close to Claude Haiku 5.5 in internal blind tests at roughly 18x lower cost per task. It is a 224B-parameter mixture-of-experts model activating 12B per token, natively multimodal with a 1M-token context, priced at $0.07 per million input tokens.

### [Google's Ask Maps can now build trip itineraries](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-holiday-travel-features-2026/)
*(2 newsletters)*

The Gemini-powered assistant in Google Maps now builds step-by-step itineraries with real-time traffic, business hours, and stop suggestions, and can find local appointments. It is rolling out now and expanding to 30 more countries in Europe, alongside Google's [holiday traffic tips](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/holiday-thanksgiving-travel-trends-2026/).

### [Cloudflare open-sources Kitesurf, a browser engine for agents](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf-open-source)
*(2 newsletters)*

Kitesurf runs entirely on Cloudflare Workers, letting agents parse HTML and CSS, run JavaScript, and render screenshots without spinning up Chrome. It exposes a Chrome DevTools Protocol endpoint so Puppeteer and other CDP clients work against it, and it is now Apache 2.0 on GitHub for local or self-deployed use.
