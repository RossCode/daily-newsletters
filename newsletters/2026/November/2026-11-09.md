### [Jensen Huang admits affair as Nvidia board probes spy claims](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/08/technology/nvidias-investigation-jensen-huang.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

Nvidia's board ordered an independent review after reports that Jensen Huang had a relationship with Alice Huang, a customer executive US intelligence agencies concluded was working for Beijing. Huang told staff "I was unfaithful to my wife" and apologized, a month after calling their meetings completely normal. The board says it has no reason to believe secrets were compromised; Huang stays CEO and has until November 11 to answer a House GOP request for a private briefing.

### [ChatGPT Images 2.6 adds GIFs and faster generation](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/)
*(4 newsletters)*

OpenAI's Images 2.6 turns static images into short looping GIFs across ChatGPT, Codex and the API, generates up to 50% faster, and adds two API models (Flare for speed, Sunburst for detail), image markup for targeted edits and SVG export. Hands-on tests found mixed results, including several downloaded GIFs that contained only one frame.

### [Anthropic explains how Claude "dreams" to fix its memory](https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic's Lance Martin describes "dreaming," where Claude reviews past session logs to correct and reorganize its memory notes. Scaling dreaming from 2 to 128 sessions raised scores on a test set from 13% to 48%, and explaining why memory matters worked better than rules about what to write. On Pokemon, a newer model earned four badges with 10 memory files versus one badge with 413 for Sonnet 3.7.

*(2 newsletters)*
