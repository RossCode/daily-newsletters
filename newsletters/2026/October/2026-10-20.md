## Top Stories

### [Musk Pledges a SpaceX Share to Every Kid in America](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/musk-pledges-to-give-every-kid-in-america-one-of-his-spacex-shares/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Elon Musk says he will give one of his own SpaceX shares to "every kid in America" starting in 2027, apparently routed through Trump Accounts, which can reportedly accept stock donations. With roughly 73 million US children and a share price around $146, that is somewhere between $10 billion and $11 billion, or about 1% of his net worth. The Rundown frames it as a response to Anthropic's Public Benefit Fund announcement; Musk has not explained mechanics but calls it "the first of many."

### [US Bans DeepSeek From Civilian Government Systems](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjkgeq0k1wevo)
*(2 newsletters)*

The Department of Homeland Security [formally banned](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-21447.pdf) DeepSeek from civilian agency systems, giving departments 60 days to request exceptions. The ban extends to third-party tools built on DeepSeek's models, which makes it broader than it first sounds. Defense Department agencies are excluded from this particular order.

### [People Are Using AI to Make Their Smart TVs Dumb Again](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/people-are-using-ai-to-make-their-smart-tvs-dumb-again/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Developers are sharing vibe-coded tools like dumbtv and cleanyourtv that strip sponsored ads, bloatware, and tracking from Android TV and Google TV sets over Wi-Fi, and speed up launchers in the process. It is a nice example of AI lowering the bar for consumer-side tinkering. If it spreads beyond hobbyists, it threatens a smart TV ad business built on fingerprinting what is on your screen.

### [MiniMax M3.1 Flash Pushes Agent Costs Down](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m31f)
*(2 newsletters)*

Chinese lab MiniMax launched M3.1 Flash (tested publicly as "Space Bunny"), aimed at coding, computer use, and agent workflows. Pricing is $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.60 per million output, with a reported 73.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 70% lower overall agent task costs than its predecessor. API access is live now; open weights are promised later.

### [Building Is Easy, Taste Is the Differentiator](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/developing-taste)
*(2 newsletters)*

A recurring theme across TLDR Dev and TLDR Product: as working software gets cheap to produce, the scarce part is design, usability, and the final 20 to 30 percent of polish. One piece argues taste is built by studying excellent work and articulating why it succeeds; [another](https://medium.muz.li/product-design-with-ai-building-it-was-never-the-hard-part-05159682106d) says AI makes building trivial but QA, restraint on feature creep, and real usability remain hard. A related essay, [Code Is Clay Now](https://www.albertodenia.com/writing/code-is-clay-now), makes the case for exploratory iteration now that changes are cheap.
