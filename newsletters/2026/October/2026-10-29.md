## Top Stories

### [Apple's Mac week: an M6 MacBook Pro, then a touchscreen OLED model](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/apple-announces-touchscreen-macbook-pro-with-oled-display/)
*(4 newsletters)*

Apple opened its Pro and Tell week with a 14-inch MacBook Pro on the M6, claiming up to 30% faster Xcode builds than M5, faster local LLM prompt processing via more Neural Accelerators per GPU core, and 24-hour battery life, alongside a new iMac and an OLED iPad mini. Memory still tops out at 32GB, so it is a small-model machine; prices start at $1,999 and units ship November 6. A day later came the bigger reveal: a touchscreen MacBook Pro with a 2,000-nit OLED display, a Dynamic Island in place of the notch, and a 3-pound chassis. [Apple's M6 announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/).

### [Anthropic manager drops the coding interview: "Hunger beats tenure"](https://felixrieseberg.com/hunger-beats-tenure-advice-for-junior-engineers/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Felix Rieseberg, who led engineering on Claude Code Desktop, says he has removed the coding interview from his team's hiring because Claude now outperforms candidates on the short tests it relied on. His counter-intuitive take is that AI is letting newer engineers overtake more experienced coders; two newer hires prototyped the Claude Code desktop app over a weekend, and it shipped about eight days later. His advice to juniors: tie one bold idea to leadership's goals and build it as a focused prototype, since "execution isn't expensive anymore."

### [Google's Genie 4 world model lets you keep prompting a live 3D scene](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Genie 4 generates photorealistic, interactive 3D worlds with physics, synchronized audio, and real-time controls, and you can keep prompting it to change the scene as you explore. Consistency now holds for about two minutes, double Genie 3, with Gemini 3.8 Flash running alongside to plan and check frames. Google pitches it for games, education, and testing robotics agents in simulation; access is waitlisted for AI Ultra subscribers and API partners.

### [Gemini 4 Carbon retakes the benchmark crown, but the public can't use it yet](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Google's second frontier model in four weeks tops 15 of 17 benchmarks on its own charts and scores 59 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index, edging past Claude Opus 5.5, with its widest margins in long-input reasoning, hacking, and workflow automation. Access is limited to selected cyber defenders while Google tightens guardrails. Intro pricing is $2/$10 per million tokens in/out, doubling to $4/$20 after the promo period.

### [Anthropic reports early results from Claude in drug development](https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic shared early results from work with six pharma and biotech partners including Bristol Myers Squibb: Claude helped Generate Biomedicines design a more effective cancer-therapy component and cut Enveda's document review time by 89%. Most contributions so far are in early-stage development. Anthropic also launched internal programs targeting rare childhood diseases and acquired Mithril Medicine, a one-person AI biotech.

### [Liquid AI's LFM3: open on-device models around 2B parameters](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm3)
*(2 newsletters)*

LFM3 is a family of open-weight text, vision, audio, omni, and decision models built on a shared backbone. The 1.7B text model fits in about 1GB at 4-bit, supports a 256K context, and reportedly beats larger models on browser and business-app tasks, while the 2B vision model matches Gemma 4 12B across 62 benchmarks. Every model fine-tunes on a single GPU, making specialized on-device agents practical; agentic coding remains a weak spot.

### [Tailscale preparing SSH privilege-escalation fixes](https://tailscale.com/ssh-investigation-update)
*(2 newsletters)*

Tailscale is patching SSH flaws that could let authorized users exceed their assigned privileges. Until the patched client ships, it advises tightening access policies and disabling unneeded SSH forwarding. Worth a look for anyone using Tailscale SSH across client environments.
