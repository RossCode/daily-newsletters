## Top Stories

### [Mistral releases Large 4, a 1T-parameter open model nicknamed "Le Chonk"](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Mistral's new 1-trillion-parameter model claims to lead every non-Chinese open system by a wide margin, scoring 62% on Mistral's coding chart (behind China's Kimi K3 at 68%, but well ahead of Reflection AI's new U.S. open model Beam at 44%). Mistral highlights cybersecurity (a claimed top-5 global ranking) and legal work, and says it trained on just 4,000 Nvidia GPUs, two to three times fewer than Chinese rivals. Weights stay closed for a roughly three-week safety preview for cyber experts and government agencies, with public release set for Oct. 27. [TechCrunch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/mistrals-new-1t-model-aims-to-leapfrog-closed-and-open-rivals/).
