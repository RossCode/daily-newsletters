## Top Stories

### [Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, its new frontier model](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)
*(5 newsletters)*

Weeks after Gemini 4 Argon, Google shipped Gemini 4 Carbon, which now ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra. It is built for long-running engineering and security work: Google says it has worked for days at a time tracking down and patching bugs, and it reports 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro and 71.0% on CWE-bench, with zero successful cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0. Access starts with cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind program, with broader availability later at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens.

### [Apple ships its first touchscreen MacBook Pro](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/apple-launches-its-new-touchscreen-macbook-pro/)
*(3 newsletters)*

The new 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pro pairs a touch-capable PROLED XDR display with a Dynamic Island-style hub, a redesigned hinge to keep the screen steady under touch, and M5 Pro/Max chips with up to 24 hours of battery. Pricing starts at $2,899 and $3,399, with preorders open now and availability November 6. Early hands-on reviews are getting heavy social traction.

### [OpenAI lets you text your Dot agent](https://www.engadget.com/2300235/openai-now-lets-you-text-your-dot-agent/)
*(3 newsletters)*

OpenAI's Dot agents can now be reached over iMessage and RCS, and they can text you first with proactive updates using the same memory and plugins they have inside ChatGPT. The beta starts with US Pro users; only you can text your Dot, and it cannot see your other conversations.

### [GitHub Universe: Copilot Fusion and multi-repo agent projects](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/)
*(3 newsletters)*

GitHub turned Copilot into an agent manager: Copilot projects run agents across multiple repos tracked in one place, and Copilot Fusion routes each task to the best model mix, which GitHub says is 40% faster at up to 77% lower cost. Enterprise customers can now use Copilot features without a separate subscription, billed through AI credits, and Microsoft made its [Copilot harness](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/28/introducing-the-microsoft-copilot-harness-unlocking-the-potential-of-truly-multi-model-agentic-work/) available through Foundry for building long-running agents.

### [Vercel CI launches in beta to keep up with coding agents](https://vercel.com/blog/introducing-vercel-ci)
*(3 newsletters)*

Agents open far more PRs than humans, and most pipelines rerun everything on every push. Vercel CI uses Turborepo-based task selection and remote caching to run only work affected by a change, sizing compute per task; Vercel reports 2 to 6x faster push-to-green times, with checks surfacing in GitHub so branch protection still works.

### [DoorDash to buy Instacart for $17 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/doordash-to-acquire-instacart-for-17b/)
*(2 newsletters)*

DoorDash agreed to acquire Instacart at $65 a share, a 26% premium to its October 22 price, with both boards already approving. Instacart keeps its brand and app, and the companies run separately until the deal closes, expected late next year or in 2028.

### [NYT: Nvidia's CEO was the target of an alleged Chinese spy](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/us/politics/nvidia-china-jensen-huang-trump.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

US officials concluded that Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore data center firm Megaspeed, was allegedly a Chinese intelligence asset while maintaining close ties to Jensen Huang; Trump was briefed about a year ago and no defensive briefing followed. Megaspeed, already under investigation for steering Nvidia chips to China, bought at least $4.8B in Nvidia gear. Nothing has been publicly proven, but the likeliest fallout is closing the loophole that lets Southeast Asian firms rent Nvidia compute to Chinese labs.

### [Reddit is moving to a logged-in-only experience](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/as-search-traffic-dries-up-reddit-announces-its-shifting-to-a-logged-in-experience/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Reddit will start testing login walls in select non-US markets this quarter, aiming to keep AI scrapers out and sell licensing instead. The backdrop: search referrals fell by more than 1 million a week in October, and US daily users slipped to 50.7 million from 53.2 million.

### [Mistral releases open weights for its trillion-parameter model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk," is now downloadable so businesses and governments can run it on their own hardware. Mistral says it matches China's GLM-5.3 on cyber work while using 47% fewer tokens per task, and it is pitched for agentic coding.

### [NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe goes open-weight](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-transcribe-granary)
*(2 newsletters)*

NVIDIA's new transcription model handles 40 languages and up to an hour of audio, with prompts for verbatim or cleaned-up text, speaker labels, timestamps, translation, and audio Q&A. It tops several transcription and speaker-attribution leaderboards, and the companion Granary v2 adds fine-tuning pipelines for specialized vocabularies.

### [Anthropic adds spend reporting for Claude Enterprise](https://claude.com/resources/articles/smart-reports-surveys-pooled-budgets)
*(2 newsletters)*

New Smart reports show what teams get done with Claude and what that work costs, alongside admin surveys and pooled group budgets with per-member limits as a guardrail. It targets the top enterprise worry about AI spend, which [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/anthropic-just-launched-tools-to-show-companies-what-their-ai-spending-is-buying/) frames as protecting Anthropic's sticky enterprise position against OpenAI's Astra.
