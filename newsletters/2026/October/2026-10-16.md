## Top Stories

### [Meta releases Muse Spark Open, built with Apple to run on Macs](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open)
*(5 newsletters)*

Meta released Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (42B active) with a 1M-token context window that takes text, images, video, and audio and is aimed at coding and agentic work. Meta tuned it with Apple so it runs locally on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios (about 1TB combined memory) via MLX, and calls it the most capable US open-weight model; on Artificial Analysis it scores 39, ahead of Western open models but behind China's GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, and Qwen3.8, which still code better. It is the third major Western open release this month after Reflection AI's Beam and Mistral's Le Chonk, and comes days after Meta's closed frontier model Muse Wonder.

### [Amazon's Bee launches two always-listening AI wearables](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/amazons-bee-launches-two-new-personal-ai-devices-starting-at-75/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Bee Frontier ($74.99, wrist or clip) and Bee Eclipse ($99.99, a card-sized slab that magnets to the back of a phone) record conversations and turn them into summaries and to-dos, integrating with Alexa and productivity apps. Fourteen days of memory are free, with $11.99/month for more; Amazon says a light shows when it is listening and recordings are not stored long-term. These are Bee's first devices since Amazon acquired it.

### [AI labs are fighting a wave of corporate espionage](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/ai-companies-play-cat-and-mouse-with-secret-stealing-spies-160eec6b)
*(3 newsletters)*

The WSJ reports that AI labs are training staff to watch for friendly strangers, eavesdroppers, and even romantic "honeypot" approaches as attempts to steal trade secrets intensify. It follows the arrest of an OpenAI employee accused of planting a hidden recording device in a conference room.

### [Google ships Gemini 4 Argon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Gemini 4 Argon is now available to Gemini Pro and Ultra users, Antigravity Ultra subscribers, and the API, with a [developer guide](https://aistudio.google.com/learn/gemini-4-argon-developer-guide) that recommends aligning on goals before long runs, giving the model a way to test its own work, and delegating smaller jobs to Flash subagents. A new Long Decode mode allows completions up to 1M tokens by splitting generation across inferences. Not everyone is sold: one senior dev YouTuber's blind audit called it a liability for real code.

### [xAI's Grok Voice 3.0 tops speech-to-speech benchmarks](https://x.ai/news/grok-voice-3)
*(3 newsletters)*

Grok Voice 3.0 leads Artificial Analysis's Speech-to-Speech Index (86.9) with sub-second latency, about 40% lower than OpenAI's GPT-Live-1, and scores 74% on ServiceNow's EVA-Bench for support-call agents. It is a drop-in upgrade on the same endpoint and $0.08/minute price, and agents pinned to `grok-voice-latest` roll over automatically. Hosted API only, no open weights.

### [TypeSafe's Jev 1.14 adds image input and longer context](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14)
*(3 newsletters)*

Jev 1.14 brings experimental image support, a new "box" type that returns a single bounding box, and longer context, all behind API "danger zone" flags; multimodal is also enabled on 1.13. TypeSafe is candid that both models do worse on images than text and that the new features are not yet reliable enough for production automation.
