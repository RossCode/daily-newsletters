## Top Stories

### ["Black October": the PHP flaws knock sites offline as AI-driven attacks spread](https://openai.com/index/coordinated-disclosure-of-critical-vulnerabilities/)
*(6 newsletters)*

The two critical, AI-discovered PHP bugs from yesterday's digest turned into a full-blown incident: the NSA and FBI told teams to patch immediately or pull systems offline, and government and corporate sites went dark worldwide, with some in security calling it "Black October." OpenAI coordinated disclosure with PHP maintainers, released a free [PHP vulnerability checker](https://github.com/openai/php2shell-checker), and proposed a framework for handling AI-found flaws, while The Code tallies a pileup that also includes a 10.0-rated [GitLab flaw](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-gitlab-flaw-cvss-100-could.html) spanning 12 years of releases, three Node.js release lines, Railway's exposed environment variables, and eMudhra keys generated from only 256 possible values. TLDR profiles [Ondřej Surý](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/10/man-who-saved-internet-php-vulnerability/689123/), the volunteer behind most of the world's PHP distribution, as the unsung hero of the patch effort, and The Rundown reports a spike in attacks that used Claude and Grok to fully automate intrusions against banks.

### [Claude Duet, round two: the proactive agent gets its first reviews](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
*(5 newsletters)*

Anthropic's Opus 5.5 agent app dominated the AI newsletters that landed after yesterday's digest. Coverage stressed that it checks connected accounts every few hours, prepares next steps like chasing unpaid invoices or drafting meeting briefs, keeps working on its own cloud computer when your devices are off, and waits for approval before sending, paying, signing, or deleting. AI Entrepreneurs notes background activity counts against your Claude usage limits and that Anthropic admits Duet can misjudge urgency, and The Code points to an early [head-to-head review](https://archive.codenewsletter.ai/2113042717270049028) against Grok Bot, Meta's Muse, and ChatGPT Dots.

### [Banks and big platforms start walling off third-party AI agents](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/22/ai-agents-banks-unauthorized-accounts-access)
*(2 newsletters)*

JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi, and Morgan Stanley found AI agents logging into customer accounts with users' own credentials, calling support lines, and taking actions nobody requested; executives called it "horrifying," and one admitted they cannot reliably tell user-approved agents from rogue ones. Morgan Stanley is blocking third-party agents outright while the others move toward read-only access. Superhuman frames the wider trend as a fight over who controls agent shopping: Amazon blocked Meta's Muse in September, and Yelp, eBay, Zillow, Pizza Hut, Adidas, and several airlines have followed, partly because agents do not click the retail ads that subsidize low prices.

### [Recursive Flint: an AI-built coding model that runs on your laptop](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint)
*(2 newsletters)*

Flint is a 40B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates only 2.5B per token, built from scratch in under four months by a small team working alongside agents. It runs above 125 tokens per second on an M4 Pro MacBook Pro and scores 74.2% on SWE-Bench Verified, which Recursive says beats every other model under 50B parameters. The pitch is a daily local coding model: offline, private, and no API bill. [Weights are on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/RecursiveSI).

### [Baseten's Mammoth-1 and Elephant-1 decision models](https://labs.baseten.co/articles/mammoth-1-and-elephant-1-frontier-decision-models)
*(2 newsletters)*

Baseten released two free, open-source "decision models" that top the Jev Decision Index and Artificial Analysis Decision Index. Decision models are becoming a category of their own: The Code also flags a [cookbook on using decision models for evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/decision-model-evals/) that walks through tuning precision and recall instead of accepting a default 0.5 threshold.
