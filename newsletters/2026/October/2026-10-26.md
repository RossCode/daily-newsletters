## Top Stories

### ["Black October": PHP flaws and AI-driven attacks swamp defenders](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/critical-php-rce-vulnerabilities-patch-now)
*(6 newsletters)*

Two critical PHP remote code execution flaws (CVE-2026-109618/109619) affecting every version since PHP 5 leaked before patches were fully out, and exploit attempts scaled from thousands to millions within hours, pushing sites like Wikipedia and Archive.org offline; patch to 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 or 8.5.12, since 8.1 and earlier get no fix. Amazon says it saw [PHP2Shell exploitation before public disclosure](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/amazon-threat-intelligence-identifies-php2shell-exploitation-prior-to-public-disclosure-in-madpot/), Microsoft says China-linked attackers used an open-source AI tool to breach ConnectWise and try 2,250+ customer environments, and US banks are finding third-party agents acting inside customer accounts. Anthropic's deputy security chief told defenders to brace for 18 rough months, and eight [eMudhra intermediate certificates](https://blog.cloudflare.com/emudhra-certificate-compromise/) were also compromised.

### [1Password for Agents lets AI log in without seeing passwords](https://1password.com/blog/1password-for-ai-agents)
*(5 newsletters)*

Agents request credentials from a user's vault, the user approves, and 1Password autofills the login without exposing the password to the model. It works with Claude Duet, ChatGPT Work, Grok Bot, Meta's Muse and others, and is free for Individual and Family accounts on 1Password.com. The Rundown already has a [step-by-step guide](https://app.therundown.ai/guides/1password-ai-agents-guide) for testing it safely with a throwaway account.

### [How the Claude Code team reviews 30,000 changes a day](https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/)
*(3 newsletters)*

With Claude writing about 90% of Claude Code, Anthropic now has agents review every PR first and tiers human review by risk: docs can merge on checks alone, standard changes need one reviewer, billing and CI need two, and whoever prompted the agent is always a required reviewer. Merged PRs more than doubled while the revert rate fell over 40%. The same scale pushed Anthropic to [replace Git with its own Scion system](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-anthropic-builds-its-own-source-control-system/) as CI hit 25 million checkouts a day.

### [The AI cost squeeze: routing, budgets, and cheaper models](https://www.onlycfo.io/p/ai-tokenomics)
*(5 newsletters)*

Token prices keep falling but bills keep rising. OpenRouter says open-weight models carry 60% of tokens but only 26% of spend, and budgeting should be per successful task, not per request. SAP's CTO named the two big wastes as sending everything to frontier models and context bloat; Harness launched [enforced weekly AI budgets](https://www.harness.io/blog/introducing-ai-budgets) that cut per-developer spend about 37%, and AI21 cut [GitLab Duo review cost 98%](https://www.ai21.com/blog/gitlab-case-study/) by switching models and compacting context.

### [Canva Craft debuts at No. 4 among image models](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Canva's first in-house image model was trained on millions of designer-made templates and plans layout before rendering, so outputs convert into editable designs. It ranks 4th on DesignArena behind only three OpenAI models, and is live in Canva and via public API, with MCP access coming.

### [Anthropic's Claude Duet enters research preview](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
*(3 newsletters)*

Duet is a proactive, Opus 5.5-powered agent app for professionals and small businesses that runs in the background in its own VM and browser, sends daily briefs, and monitors connected apps. The same weekend, two of Claude Code's most visible builders announced exits ahead of Anthropic's IPO.

### [Apple's iPhone Duo stress tests go viral](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8pCccpKjjos)
*(4 newsletters)*

Reviews peg the foldable as a pocket tablet best for commuters and travelers, and otherwise an average phone. A 10,000-fold stress test showing the screen eventually creasing has drawn 52M+ views and shaken some pre-order plans.

### [Meta ordered to pay New Mexico $25B over Cambridge Analytica](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-ordered-pay-25-billion-new-mexico-after-jury-finds-company-misled-consumers-2026-10-21/)
*(2 newsletters)*

A jury found nearly 44M consumer-protection violations from 2010 to 2021; the judge cut a potential $219.5B penalty to $25B, five times the FTC's 2019 fine, with proceeds going to education. Meta will appeal. Separately, Meta [proposed a nonvoting share class](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/meta-wants-to-pay-for-its-broad-ai-buildout-with-a-newly-created-class-of-stock/) to fund $825B in commitments.

### [Claude-assisted formal verification finds bugs testing missed](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)
*(2 newsletters)*

Claude produced machine-checked proofs for 239 fuzz-test entry points across 123 open-source libraries' RISC-V code and surfaced bugs conventional testing had missed. The guarantees cover specific security properties under stated assumptions, not full correctness.

### [Oura sues Ultrahuman again](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/oura-sues-rival-ultrahuman-again-threatening-its-u-s-smart-ring-comeback/)
