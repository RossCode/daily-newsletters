## Top Stories

### [Anthropic's S-1 Shows Red-Hot Growth, an $80B Loss, and a Huge Compute Bill](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/nscale-and-anthropic-are-reportedly-in-talks-for-yet-another-45-billion-compute-deal/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Anthropic's draft IPO prospectus reportedly shows a net loss of more than $80 billion in the first half of the year alongside breakneck revenue growth, with risk factors covering model harms, liability, and uncertain access to compute. The company has signaled it could spend $750 billion on compute over the next few years, and it is reportedly in talks with Nscale on a second $45 billion deal just a month after the first. Both companies are pursuing IPOs, with Anthropic still expected to list this year. ([S-1 coverage](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/7b18rV))

### [Claude Designs Protein Binders Against Scorpion Neurotoxins](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design)
*(2 newsletters)*

Working with Citrini Research, an internal Anthropic model ran for 64 hours off five human messages, generated 318,484 candidate designs, and sent 211 to outside labs; 17 passed binding tests. Each binder grabs only one toxin and none has been shown to neutralize venom, so Citrini called its universal-antivenom moonshot a failure while arguing the frontier for AI drug discovery has moved. The notable part is who ran it: a five-person investment research team with roughly a $200K budget and no deep drug development background.

### [Salesforce Buys Braze for $5.66B in Cash](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/salesforce-braze-acquisition-deal.html)
*(2 newsletters)*

Salesforce will pay $47.50 per share in cash for Braze (about $5.7B including debt), adding a customer engagement platform built for personalized, AI-driven outreach. Braze stock jumped 51% on the news. The deal is not expected to close until fiscal 2028.

### [OpenAI Acquires Orchestra Bio for Its Life Sciences Push](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-orchestra-bio/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Orchestra Bio's agents handle drug development chores like study tracking, finances, and clinical trial scheduling, and the team will join work on Rosalind, OpenAI's life sciences model. Terms were not disclosed. OpenAI says Moderna and Novo Nordisk are already Rosalind customers, which makes this one more sign that frontier labs are building vertical science businesses rather than just selling models.

### [Inside Meta's Gigawatt-Scale Prometheus Supercluster](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/10/19/data-center-engineering/inside-prometheus-meta-gigawatt-scale-supercluster/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Meta says phase one of Prometheus in Ohio is complete, linking hundreds of thousands of Nvidia GPUs across buildings and storm-proof tents, with plans to reach about one million. At its worst in mid-2025 the cluster hit roughly 1,000 interruptions per 8,000 GPUs per day; Meta and Nvidia cut that by 99%, partly by running 64 of every 72 chips per rack and keeping 8 as hot spares. Training pauses can swing power draw by tens of megawatts in seconds, so Meta caps GPU power in software and absorbs spikes with batteries.

### [OpenAI Reports a Big Jump in COBOL Migration](https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/PvvPA4)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI introduced evals for building software across 20 languages and migrating COBOL while preserving behavior. GPT-6.1 Sol hit 86.6% on its COBOL migration benchmark at low reasoning effort, versus 5.4% for GPT-5.6 Sol, with gains also reported for Fortran and Visual Basic. For enterprise modernization work, that is the difference between a demo and a plausible delivery tool.

### [The Agent Governance Problem Comes Into Focus](https://www.cio.com/article/4236549/the-agents-nobody-meant-to-build.html)
*(3 newsletters)*

CIO warns about unsanctioned agents that employees create by accident with approved desktop tools and recurring workflows, citing a Gartner forecast that 40% of enterprises will demote or shut down agents by 2027 over governance gaps. Microsoft published guidance on [securing the "agentic insider"](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/19/securing-the-agentic-insider/), focused on agents holding more access than their tasks need. Anthropic added a [cookbook for auditing finished agent sessions](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/managed-agents-cma-agent-security-review) for prompt injection, leaked credentials, unauthorized hosts, and retried blocked calls, producing a ranked report for human review.

### ["Unlearning" Becomes the AI-Era Skill](https://www.techradar.com/pro/enterprise-ai-outcomes-depend-on-the-art-of-unlearning)
*(2 newsletters)*

Google VP Josh Woodward says he now screens candidates for the ability to unlearn, looking for people who learned something new and then pivoted. TechRadar makes the same argument at the org level: enterprise AI stalls when companies ignore change management, and success depends on helping people drop old software habits, backed by roles like forward deployed engineers and agentic design leads.

### [AWS Releases TorchNeuron, a Native PyTorch Backend for Trainium](https://pytorch.org/blog/introducing-torchneuron-native-pytorch-backend-for-aws-trainium/)
*(2 newsletters)*

TorchNeuron lets existing PyTorch model code, libraries, and tools run on Trainium with minimal changes and supports eager execution for research and debugging. It is in public beta under Apache 2.0. It is developer infrastructure, not a price cut, but it lowers the switching cost away from Nvidia for teams training their own models.
