## Top Stories

### [Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)
*(4 newsletters)*

Anthropic released Opus 5 across the Claude apps, Claude Code, and the API at $5 and $25 per million tokens, the same pricing as 4.8. The pitch is near-Fable-5 intelligence at roughly half the cost, with state of the art results on agentic terminal coding, agentic search, and computer use, a 42 out of 42 on International Math Olympiad 2026 problems, and 30.2% on ARC-AGI-3, about three times the next best model. It also ships a Fast mode running roughly 2.5x quicker, and Anthropic describes it as its most aligned model to date. The practical shift is that frontier-tier capability becomes cheap enough to use as a daily driver instead of reserving it for the hardest work.

### [ChatGPT Health opens to all US users](https://openai.com/index/health-in-chatgpt/)
*(4 newsletters)*

OpenAI broadened Health in ChatGPT to US adults, letting people connect Apple Health and supported medical records so the model can explain lab results, compare them against earlier tests, summarize changes since the last appointment, and prep questions using medication, sleep, and visit history as context. OpenAI says connected health data and related chats are not used to train foundation models or target ads, and that users control access per session. The company claims the latest models reason above clinician level on some tasks while insisting the product supports rather than replaces care.

### [Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta and 47 others sign an open letter defending open-weight models](https://x.com/JensenHuang/status/2080643682408321103)
*(5 newsletters)*

Jensen Huang posted an open letter urging Washington not to restrict open-weight AI models, and within a day the signatory list roughly doubled to include OpenAI, Google, AMD, and Cisco. Anthropic is the conspicuous holdout. The letter argues open weights expand access, preserve competition, broaden cyber defense, and prevent vendor lock-in, and it warns against confusing distillation with misappropriation. This lands amid US accusations that Chinese labs distilled American models, and with open-weight models now estimated to be used in 80% of startups and the frontier gap compressed to four to six months.

### [Black Forest Labs launches FLUX 3](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3)
*(4 newsletters)*

FLUX 3 is a unified multimodal model generating images plus audio-video clips up to 20 seconds from a single prompt, with action prediction built in. A version is already driving real-world robots deployed at Audi through a partnership with mimic. FLUX 3 Video is in early access with broader availability limited to start. The interesting part is less the media quality than the claim that world-model pretraining on video transfers to robotic perception and control.

### [OpenAI and Anthropic both ship major voice upgrades](https://www.engadget.com/2221938/claude-voice-mode-just-got-smarter/)
*(3 newsletters)*

The two labs shipped competing voice updates on the same day, in different directions. ChatGPT's desktop app now lets paid users drive agents and Codex by voice with full duplex GPT-Live audio on macOS and Windows. Claude's voice mode moved to Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku for better conversational reasoning and can now connect to Gmail, Slack, Notion, and Google Calendar mid-conversation, with free accounts limited to Haiku and one connected app.

### [AMD unveils Helios and lands Anthropic and Microsoft](https://www.amd.com/en/corporate/events/advancing-ai.html)
*(3 newsletters)*

AMD introduced its Helios rack-scale platform alongside Instinct MI400 GPUs and new EPYC CPUs, claiming up to 30% more inference tokens per dollar than competing systems. Microsoft will deploy Helios on Azure for frontier-model inference, and Anthropic committed to up to two gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs starting in 2027 under a partnership worth up to $5B, including Claude optimization for AMD hardware and expanded ROCm support. AMD also announced a joint ultra-low-latency inference stack with Cerebras. This is the first genuinely credible multi-front challenge to Nvidia's position.

### [Google Cloud revenue jumps 82% on enterprise AI demand](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/google-justifies-its-massive-ai-spending-with-a-booming-cloud-business/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Google Cloud posted $24.8B in quarterly revenue with backlog climbing to $514B, while Alphabet overall grew 24% to $119.8B. Alphabet attributed the cloud growth largely to AI infrastructure and enterprise AI products. For anyone watching whether hyperscaler AI capex actually converts to revenue, this is the strongest datapoint so far.

### [Waymo is reportedly looking to exit its Uber deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/waymo-reportedly-mulling-a-breakup-with-uber/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Waymo is said to be seeking a way out of the arrangement that puts its robotaxis on Uber's network in Austin and Atlanta. Waymo plans to run robotaxis through its own app in those markets starting in January, with the Uber contract running to May. Separately, an independent study found Waymo vehicles involved in fewer crashes, and notably fewer injury crashes, than human drivers.
