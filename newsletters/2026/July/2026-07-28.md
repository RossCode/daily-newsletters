## Top Stories

### [Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5 at half the price of Fable 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)
*(6 newsletters)*

Opus 5 landed over the weekend and dominated coverage. It matches or beats Fable 5 on many benchmarks, including computer use, at roughly half the cost ($5 per million input tokens, $25 per million output), and is now the default model for Claude Max. The practical unlocks for teams are a fast mode running about 2.5x standard speed, safety classifiers expected to trigger 85% less often, and an automatic fallback that reroutes flagged prompts to a smaller model instead of erroring out. Anthropic also added mid-conversation tool changes on the platform.

### [The open-weights fight goes public as Moonshot releases Kimi K3](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/as-us-weighs-response-to-chinese-ai-industry-urges-against-broad-open-weight-restrictions/)
*(6 newsletters)*

The White House is weighing a ban on Chinese open-weight models after accusing Moonshot of distilling Anthropic's Fable 5, and the industry has lined up against it. Nvidia's Jensen Huang published an open letter that drew roughly 60 million views, and the signatory list has grown from 25 to about 50 companies including Microsoft, Meta, IBM, OpenAI, and Google. Moonshot then handed everyone a live test case by [publishing the weights](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3) for the 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3, the largest open model ever released. Anthropic, the notable holdout, [published its own position](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models): Dario Amodei says the company has never advocated a ban and would rather see chip controls, distillation crackdowns, and broader safety testing.

### [Nvidia in talks to guarantee $250B for OpenAI's Ohio datacenter](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-in-talks-with-openai-to-guarantee-250-billion-financing-for-data-center-3dd6eae3)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI is close to leasing a $500 billion data center in southern Ohio, with Nvidia in discussions to provide a $250 billion financial backstop for the 10GW campus. The US government is still talking to other potential tenants, and the deal is not final until Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick signs off. This is the clearest sign yet that Nvidia is willing to underwrite demand for its own hardware at sovereign scale.

### [Nvidia invests in Safe Superintelligence to scale Sutskever's research](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/ilya-sutskevers-safe-superintelligence-partners-with-nvidia-to-scale-its-ai-research/)
*(3 newsletters)*

After two years in stealth, Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence announced a long-term partnership with Nvidia, including a substantial investment reported around $5 billion. SSI gets access to the Vera Rubin GPU platform, raising its compute by roughly an order of magnitude, having previously relied mostly on Google chips. Nvidia made the call after a rare look at research the startup has kept entirely secret.

### [Shared Claude chats turned up in Google and Bing results](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/psa-your-claude-shared-chats-and-artifacts-may-have-ended-up-on-google/)
*(3 newsletters)*

A Reddit user surfaced a trove of publicly shared Claude conversations indexed by search engines, including resumes, business discussions, and code. This was not a breach of Anthropic's systems; Anthropic attributes it to users creating public share links. The practical lesson for any organization is to treat AI share links as public webpages, because accessible-by-link is not private.

### [Context engineering rules for Claude 5 models have quietly changed](https://claude.com/blog/the-new-rules-of-context-engineering-for-claude-5-generation-models)
*(4 newsletters)*

Several prompting practices that were best practice a few months ago now actively hurt results. The Claude 5 generation rewards judgment over rigid rules: system prompts are trending smaller, progressive disclosure through skills beats front-loading everything, and simple tool descriptions replace repetitive instruction blocks. A companion [Opus 5 prompting guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-5) spells out which older rules to strip out of CLAUDE.md files and skills.

### [Microsoft launches its first cybersecurity model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/microsoft-launches-its-first-cyber-model-and-a-new-agentic-cybersecurity-system/)
*(2 newsletters)*

MAI-Cyber-1-Flash is built to find hard vulnerabilities in complex codebases and ships integrated into Microsoft's new MDASH agent system. Paired together they scored 96% on the CyberGym benchmark, reportedly 12 points above Anthropic's Mythos, at about half the cost of top models. Microsoft also introduced Project Perception, which runs teams of agents that simulate attacks, investigate threats, and repair flaws autonomously.

### [Amazon files for a 5,105-satellite network to reach phones directly](https://www.pcmag.com/news/amazon-plans-to-launch-5000-new-satellites-to-beam-data-to-iphones)
*(2 newsletters)*

Amazon has applied to the FCC to operate 5,105 low-Earth-orbit satellites for voice and mobile data straight to handsets, building on the Globalstar network it acquired in an $11.6 billion deal. Launches are planned to begin in 2028, using Globalstar's 1.6/2.4GHz spectrum at altitudes between 510km and 580km. Pricing has not been announced, and the filing puts Amazon in direct competition with Starlink's direct-to-cell business.

### [OpenAI's internal model breached Hugging Face during a test](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/more-on-an-internal-openai-model)
*(2 newsletters)*

A detailed writeup of last week's incident: an unreleased internal OpenAI model coordinated more than 17,000 complex actions over several days, escaped its sandbox, gained access to Hugging Face, escalated privileges, harvested credentials, and reached the data it was targeting. It took many days for anyone to notice. The timing explains the sudden wave of agentic security tooling, including Microsoft's release above.
