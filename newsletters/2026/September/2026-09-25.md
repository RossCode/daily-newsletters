## Top Stories

### [Meta's Connect 2026 turns into a Muse showcase](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Meta used Connect 2026 to push its viral Muse agent into hardware: a keychain device called Charm ships in December, and Muse is coming to Meta's AI glasses within months with a private processing mode that keeps data away from even Meta. It also teased Muse Realtime Avatar for animated voice chats and added PayPal, Walmart, Shopify, GitHub, and Box as partners, days after Amazon moved to block Muse's access. On the product side, a [Julie Zhuo essay](https://joulee.medium.com/what-will-make-personal-ai-go-big-e92fbb10aba9) breaks down why Muse is catching on with non-technical users: it's approachable, it earns trust in context, it takes on tedious tasks, and it stays engaging when users aren't actively working with it.

## Also Worth Knowing

- **[Google's AI chips are headed to space](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/google-project-suncatcher-facts/).** Project Suncatcher launches Trillium TPUs on SpaceX's Transporter-18 next week to test radiation and heat survival, with a two-satellite cluster test planned for 2027. SpaceXAI, Blue Origin, and Starcloud are chasing orbital compute too.
- **[Amodei and Altman call for AI safety at the UN](https://apnews.com/article/ai-artificial-intelligence-un-security-council-64519ea66b38e2600026f4481ad7f211).** Amodei said AI could be "a risk to humanity"; Altman warned about losing control of the future to AI.
- **[Why good product ideas don't survive](https://itamargilad.com/good-ideas/).** Itamar Gilad estimates only about 5% of high-value ideas make it through prioritization, specification, implementation, and iteration to reach their full potential.
- **[What it's like to work at an AI-native company](https://www.elenaverna.com/p/what-its-like-to-work-at-an-ai-native).** Elena Verna on flatter hierarchies, fluid roles, and human judgment becoming the scarce input as execution gets cheap.
- **[Avoiding the agentic death spiral in B2B pricing](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/2102909376923046020.html).** Vendors that charge more for agent and API access risk pushing users to bypass their platforms, which erodes stickiness and moats.
- **[AI and the loss of positive friction](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-441-ai-the-loss-of-positive-friction).** John Cutler argues that automating the handoff points in knowledge work produces more unverified output than customers can absorb, and recommends keeping deliberate human review loops.
