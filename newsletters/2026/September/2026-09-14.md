## Top Stories

### [The people building AI are publicly asking to slow it down](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)
*(5 newsletters)*

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay arguing that labs should deliberately pace frontier capability gains so safety work can catch up, citing recursive self-improvement as the core risk and proposing third-party evaluators embedded inside labs. In an unusual show of alignment, Sam Altman committed to evaluators and Elon Musk, Demis Hassabis, and Satya Nadella all backed the direction, while Trump [dismissed](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/trump-says-very-negative-forces-raising-exaggerated-concerns-over-ai-2026-09-13/) slowdown advocates as negative forces. The week's backdrop was grim: a viral resignation post from researcher Jacob Coxon accusing both Anthropic and OpenAI of gambling with lives, Anthropic alignment lead Evan Hubinger [putting the odds of AI killing all humans above 10%](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-kill-humans-anthropic-researcher-more-than-ten-percent-chance/) within a decade, OpenAI board member Paul Christiano [warning](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/10/openai-risk-catastrophic-loss-control-board-member-paul-christiano) the industry is not on track to prevent loss of control, and two safety researchers [leaving](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/security/two-ai-researchers-leave-anthropic-google-safety-concerns-rcna597086) Anthropic and Google DeepMind for METR.

### [OpenAI opens up the harness behind Codex](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/)
*(4 newsletters)*

The Agents API is now in public beta, giving developers the same managed agent infrastructure that powers Codex through a single call. It handles context across multiple windows, tool use, parallel subagents, file and code environments, and persistent execution for agents that run for days, with no fee beyond tokens and tools consumed. Developers can run agents in OpenAI-hosted sandboxes or their own infrastructure, and the API supports MCP tools plus partners like Vercel and Cloudflare.

### [Personal agents become the category everyone is chasing](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)

Meta's Muse, a personal agent that plugs into email, calendars, payments, and apps for health, shopping, dining, and music, has rocketed to second place on the app charts and will reportedly gain a [Shared Agents feature](https://www.testingcatalog.com/meta-to-announce-shared-agents-for-muse-at-meta-connect/) at Meta Connect that lets users build and distribute custom agents. ### [Coding models are getting frontier-class results at a fraction of frontier prices](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2)
*(3 newsletters)*

Cognition's SWE-2 hit 50.0% on FrontierCode 1.1 while running 64% cheaper, beating Grok 4.6 on both score and cost, matching GPT-5.6 Sol and Fable 5.1 at a fraction of their price, and landing within a few points of GPT-6 Astra at a quarter of the cost. DeepSeek's open-weight [V4.1-Flash](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash) makes a similar argument from the other direction: a 552B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates only 8B parameters for input and 16B for output at $0.30 per million input tokens. The practical read is that the cost floor for competent agentic coding is dropping fast enough to change build-versus-buy math.

### [Anthropic publishes what attackers are actually doing with Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)
*(2 newsletters)*

The September threat intelligence report documents operations disrupted between December 2025 and August, spanning surveillance, weapons development, and cyberattacks. Two trends stand out: AI's role in cyberattacks is becoming more autonomous rather than merely assistive, and sophisticated attacks no longer require sophisticated attackers. None of the disrupted misuse cases involved Claude Fable or Mythos-class models.
