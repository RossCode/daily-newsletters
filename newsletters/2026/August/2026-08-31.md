## Top Stories

### [Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for roughly $13B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/)
*(5 newsletters)*

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, though reporting conflicts on whether anything is actually signed yet and neither company has commented. The deal would hand Nvidia control of the largest open-source model ecosystem and another route into cloud compute. The timing is awkward and excellent for Hugging Face: it landed the same week the company shipped a $399 open-source robot that has already crossed $1 million in sales.

### [OpenAI cuts Cursor off after the SpaceX acquisition](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/)
*(2 newsletters)*

OpenAI will remove its models from Cursor by November 12, citing Elon Musk's history of violating contracts as the reason and using cancellation rights that opened when SpaceX bought the editor. OpenAI's stated evidence includes the killed Twitter ad deal and xAI training on OpenAI outputs, which Musk called "partly" true. Musk's public response was that he could not care less, while Cursor's CEO put OpenAI's share of the platform's traffic at roughly 5 percent. Cursor's value proposition has been neutrality across every frontier model, so this is the first real crack in that.

### [Federal judge rules the Pentagon's Anthropic blacklist was illegal](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/72379655/250/anthropic-pbc-v-us-department-of-war/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Judge Rita Lin held that the Department of War's "supply chain risk" designation on Anthropic violated the First and Fifth Amendments and was retaliation for the lab's public criticism rather than a genuine security finding, calling it arbitrary and capricious. She noted the Pentagon had simultaneously floated invoking the Defense Production Act on Anthropic, which would make the company essential rather than dangerous. The label technically survives while a second case sits in appeals, and the Pentagon says its Claude wind-down finishes September 30. The precedent matters more than the outcome here.

### [Nvidia posts 106% revenue growth and calls it an inflection point](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-nvda-q2-earnings.html)
*(3 newsletters)*

Quarterly revenue hit $96.2 billion, up 106 percent year over year, and Jensen Huang guided to roughly 70 percent growth for FY2028, which would put next year near $700 billion. Analysts a year ago had FY2028 pegged around $310 billion, so the sell side has been short by well over $100 billion even while revising upward all year. Nvidia also expects demand to keep outstripping supply, which will shape enterprise pricing and procurement timelines rather than just stock charts.

### [Meta settles teen-safety claims for $16.7B to $18B](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/26/facebook-instagram-changes-key-takeaways)
*(4 newsletters)*

Meta agreed to resolve claims from dozens of states that it engineered Facebook and Instagram to be addictive to minors. Reported figures differ across sources: roughly $16.68 billion from the 29-state settlement, or about $18 billion total, of which Meta guarantees $12.7 billion and roughly $5.3 billion only lands if TikTok and YouTube adopt comparable restrictions and pay about $6 billion each. Teen accounts get a two-hour daily limit, overnight shutdown, muted school-hour notifications, and stronger age checks. Personalized recommendations and targeted ads survive untouched, and Meta has taken out full-page ads pressuring rivals to accept the same terms.

### [AWS commits to 2 million more Nvidia GPUs](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai)
*(2 newsletters)*

Amazon tripled its earlier commitment, adding another 2 million Nvidia GPUs including Blackwell Ultra and Rubin systems across 2027 and 2028, after demand blew past its first million-GPU order. The expanded partnership also brings Nvidia Vera CPUs, networking, open models, and robotics tooling into Bedrock and SageMaker. Read alongside Anthropic's separately reported six-year, $45 billion, 460MW lease with Nscale, the compute buildout is not slowing.

### [OpenAI and 130 organizations warn AI cyberattacks are about to accelerate](https://openai.com/collective-cyberdefense/)
*(3 newsletters)*

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, AWS, and CrowdStrike among others signed an open letter arguing that organizations have months, not years, to prepare for AI-enabled attacks on hospitals, utilities, governments, and internet infrastructure. The specific ask is defensive: wider access to cyber-capable models for defenders, stronger security standards, shared threat intelligence, and funding for critical infrastructure. The practical takeaway for teams is to patch the biggest exposures now and tighten review of AI-generated code.

### [Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash adds real directorial control to AI video](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-with-gemini-omni-1-1-flash/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Google shipped controls that move AI video from generation toward direction: scenes extend in 10-second increments up to 40 seconds, creators can specify first and last frames for clean transitions, 360p drafts generate up to 60 percent faster at a third the cost of 720p, and finished work upscales to 1080p or 4K. Available through the Gemini API and AI Studio. The draft-then-upscale loop is what makes this usable for repeatable production work rather than one-off demos.

### [Apple names John Ternus CEO and sets September 9 for the foldable iPhone](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/26/apple-officially-announces-iphone-18-pro-foldable-event/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Ternus, a 25-year hardware veteran, takes over from Tim Cook on September 1, with AI as the stated first priority. His opening act is a September 9 event expected to include Apple's first foldable at around $2,500, plus the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, with the regular iPhone 18 pushed to 2027. IDC expects 10 million-plus foldable units in year one and roughly 40 percent of the global foldable market by 2027. The rest of the fall lineup reportedly includes a smart display, camera-equipped AirPods, and a camera pendant.

### [Salesforce puts Claude at the center of its stack, and itself inside Claude](https://www.salesforce.com/claudeforce/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Claude is now the reasoning model behind Salesforce's Atlas Reasoning Engine and powers Agentforce Vibes and Agentforce Coworker by default. Slack runs on Claude too. The reverse direction is the more interesting half: Salesforce for Claude is a plugin giving sellers 37 prebuilt skills that run inside Anthropic's own interface rather than inside Salesforce. The announcement came with a record quarter, $11.3 billion in revenue up 11 percent, and a 20 percent stock jump that eased fears AI would eat SaaS.
