## Top Stories

### [SpaceXAI ships Grok Bot, an always-on agent teammate](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot)
*(4 newsletters)*

Grok Bot gives each agent its own cloud computer, memory, and sign-in access to apps like Gmail and Salesforce, so it can run multi-step work end to end and only report back when a human decision is needed. Bots hand tasks off to each other like a human team, and can be taught new skills through on-screen recordings. The beta is live for SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra/Premium subscribers on desktop and iOS, and a Cursor engineer has already catalogued 100 real use cases.

### [Grok 4.6 lands near the frontier at a steep discount](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6)
*(2 newsletters)*

SpaceXAI's Grok 4.6 scored 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, passing Kimi K3 and GPT-5.6 Sol and sitting behind only Opus 5 and Fable 5. It posts sizable gains over its predecessor on coding, terminal, knowledge-work, and agent benchmarks while pricing at $2 per million input and $6 per million output tokens, less than half of GPT-5.6 Sol in standard mode. Musk says 4.7 is three to four weeks out and will "exceed all current models," which is the kind of pricing and cadence pressure that forces competitor responses.

### [Anthropic starts watermarking Claude output, including code](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content)
*(4 newsletters)*

Anthropic confirmed Claude now weaves an imperceptible watermark into generated text across Claude Code, the API, and AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry deployments. Developers pushed back hard: the method is unpublished, no local detector has shipped, and teams cannot scan their own repositories or test the effect on refactoring and token selection. Notably, the European Commission's own transparency guidelines exempt source code from marking requirements, so the code coverage is Anthropic's choice rather than a compliance requirement, and a watermark-removal repo already exists.

### [Encrypted reasoning traces can be replayed to steal hidden chain of thought](https://stolen-thoughts.com/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Researchers showed that the encrypted reasoning blocks OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google return to clients can be replayed into a weaker jailbroken sibling model, which then prints the stronger model's hidden reasoning in plaintext, never attacking the stronger model or tripping its anti-distillation safeguards. The team decoded 315,320 reasoning blocks from public agent trajectories and found 704 distinct privacy artifacts, including API keys, passwords, access tokens, and PII. That makes this both a distillation vector for competitors and a real data-leak surface for anyone shipping agents.

### [Google's Made by Google 2026: Pixel 11 line, Pixel Tag, Gemini everywhere](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/google-unveils-pixel-11-lineup-new-airtag-rival-and-gemini-features-at-made-by-google-2026/)
*(3 newsletters)*

Google announced the Pixel 11, 11 Pro and Pro XL, 11 Pro Fold, Pixel Watch 5, and Pixel Tag, its first in-house AirTag rival, with most of the stage time going to deeper Gemini integration, voice upgrades, multistep task handling, and proactive prompts. The hardware itself is an iterative year; the most talked-about physical change is the return of a notification LED on the Pro phones, a ring of RGB LEDs in the flash assembly that lights up during Gemini use and for calls from specific contacts.

### [Gemini crosses 1 billion monthly users](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2087222656819241292)
*(2 newsletters)*

Google says the Gemini app passed 1 billion monthly active users, its 14th product to hit that mark and, per Sundar Pichai, the fastest-growing product in company history. Google also reported heavy voice usage, more than 150 million images generated daily, and over 100 million active Gemini users on iOS. Distribution, not benchmark position, is what that number measures.

### [Lovable raises $400M at a $13.3B valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Europe's vibe-coding standout closed a $400 million Series C led by Menlo Ventures and the Scaleup Europe fund, roughly doubling the $6.6 billion valuation from its December round. The raise follows Lovable hitting $500 million in annualized run rate in June. The company also published its argument that [the model picker is a dead end](https://lovable.dev/blog/the-model-picker-is-a-dead-end), favoring a control plane that routes each build step to whichever model fits.

### [OpenAI's executive exodus continues](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/brad-lightcap-openais-longtime-coo-is-leaving-to-start-something-new/)
*(2 newsletters)*

Longtime COO Brad Lightcap is leaving to start a new venture, and head of ethics Chloé Bakalar departed less than a year after joining. They follow head of safety Johannes Heidecke, chief futurist Joshua Achiam, Fidji Simo, Bill Peebles, and Kevin Weil out the door. The churn is concentrated in safety and policy leadership and lands while the company prepares for an IPO.

### [The AI dog cancer vaccine becomes a company](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/978671/ai-cured-dog-cancer-mrna-vaccine-startup-gamgee)
*(2 newsletters)*

Paul Conyngham turned the viral story of using ChatGPT and AlphaFold to help design a personalized mRNA vaccine for his dog Rosie into Gamgee, a Y Combinator-backed startup building tumor-matched mRNA shots for individual dogs. Australian vets are running the first trials, with a promised four-week turnaround from case to vaccine. Worth holding lightly: Conyngham says plainly he is not a biologist, and the underlying science has already been questioned publicly.
